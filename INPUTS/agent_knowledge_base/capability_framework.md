# Enterprise Capability Framework

This framework represents a realistic, evidence-based competency profile for a Senior Finance Systems Product Manager, Finance Technology Product Owner, Business Process & Technology Transformation Lead, and developing Business / Enterprise Architecture profile.

It is intended for CV positioning, LinkedIn refinement, role matching, interview preparation, capability mapping, learning planning, and skill development.

The framework distinguishes between:

* **Established capabilities**: areas supported by repeated professional application, direct ownership, leadership accountability, or long-term practical experience.
* **Established / Developing capabilities**: areas where there is strong professional exposure and practical involvement, but where specialist ownership should still be framed carefully.
* **Developing capabilities**: areas currently being strengthened through structured learning, hands-on personal projects, role-adjacent exposure, or partial professional involvement.
* **Awareness-level capabilities**: areas where conceptual understanding exists, but which should not yet be presented as direct ownership, administration, configuration, or specialist expertise unless supported by specific examples.

## 1. Product Ownership & Finance Systems Governance

* **Finance Systems Product Ownership**
  * Own and manage finance systems product scope, roadmap, backlog, priorities, governance forums, support model, risk register, change approvals, vendor delivery, and stakeholder communication.
  * Govern finance technology products across planning, budgeting, controlling, reporting, consolidation, data-flow, access, compliance, and support-process contexts.
  * Translate finance, reporting, control, technology, and governance needs into structured product decisions, roadmap items, backlog entries, risks, dependencies, and delivery priorities.
  * Coordinate across finance users, technology teams, product experts, developers, support teams, vendors, governance stakeholders, and senior leadership.
  * Balance business value, process stability, compliance, user impact, technical feasibility, cost, supportability, and delivery risk when making product and roadmap decisions.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established finance systems product ownership capability, including roadmap, backlog, product governance, vendor delivery, risk/change governance, support model, and stakeholder communication.

* **Finance Systems Product Governance**
  * Govern finance technology landscapes involving Board, SAP ERP / SAP ECC, SAP BW, SAP Analytics Cloud, SAP FC, SAP FIM, Power BI, Azure, Oracle ERP, and related reporting / planning / controlling platforms.
  * Understand how finance applications, reporting tools, data flows, integration points, access controls, and support processes interact across the product landscape.
  * Own or contribute to governance decisions around product changes, access, support, reporting impacts, risk, controls, compliance, system dependencies, and business continuity.
  * Work with specialists who configure and maintain technical components while retaining business/product accountability for priorities, outcomes, risks, and stakeholder alignment.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established finance systems product governance capability, with strong business/product ownership and architecture/data-flow understanding, while avoiding unsupported claims of hands-on configuration for every underlying platform.

* **Roadmap, Backlog and Product Strategy Ownership**
  * Own product roadmap, backlog, prioritization, product strategy, support model, governance forums, stakeholder communication, and change approval processes.
  * Translate strategic objectives, operational pain points, compliance needs, reporting gaps, technical dependencies, and user requirements into roadmap and backlog priorities.
  * Make or support decisions on sequencing, trade-offs, prioritization, risk mitigation, benefits, resources, vendor delivery, and implementation dependencies.
  * Maintain alignment between finance stakeholders, technology teams, vendors, support teams, governance functions, and leadership.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established product strategy, roadmap, backlog, and governance ownership for finance systems and enterprise technology products.

## 2. Strategy & Transformation

* **Transformation Analysis & Roadmap Support**
  * Analyze current-state business processes, system dependencies, delivery risks, operational gaps, stakeholder concerns, governance issues, and financial impacts to support transformation planning.
  * Structure transformation topics into clear problem statements, target directions, dependencies, risks, milestones, benefits, costs, and proposed next steps.
  * Support and lead roadmap discussions by clarifying what needs to change, why it matters, what dependencies exist, and what decisions are required.
  * Contribute to migration-path, product-roadmap, impact, benefit, cost, control, and milestone discussions across finance systems, reporting, governance, access, support, and technology-management contexts.
  * Translate complex enterprise change topics into structured updates that support decision-making, prioritization, stakeholder alignment, and delivery governance.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established capability in transformation analysis, roadmap structuring, and finance systems change governance.

* **Transformation Leadership and Transition Management**
  * Lead or support transformation initiatives across finance, reporting, technology, operational support, system transition, process standardization, and service-delivery contexts.
  * Manage transition topics involving people, process, technology, documentation, governance, controls, support model, handover, business continuity, and stakeholder readiness.
  * Coordinate cross-functional teams through change, including finance experts, technology specialists, developers, vendors, support teams, and governance stakeholders.
  * Identify risks, dependencies, readiness gaps, operating model impacts, and process issues during transformation or transition.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established transformation and transition leadership capability across finance operations, finance systems, reporting, product governance, and service-delivery environments.

* **Operating Model & Ways-of-Working Improvement**
  * Analyze how governance routines, ownership models, decision rights, communication channels, approval flows, escalation paths, service models, and support processes influence operational effectiveness.
  * Implement and improve ways of working across ITSM, SDLC, agile delivery, operational support, product governance, change management, incident management, and stakeholder communication.
  * Define or improve triage, backlog handling, assessment steps, acceptance criteria, escalation routes, severity handling, support handover, release governance, and closure routines.
  * Clarify roles, responsibilities, governance forums, hypercare arrangements, continuity processes, and product/support ownership.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established capability in operating model and ways-of-working improvement, including ITSM, SDLC, agile, support model, and finance technology governance processes.

* **Continuous Improvement & Operational Excellence**
  * Identify opportunities to simplify, standardize, document, automate, control, and improve recurring finance, reporting, technology, governance, and support processes.
  * Analyze manual, spreadsheet-heavy, approval-driven, governance-heavy, and cross-functional workflows to identify inefficiencies, risks, duplication, rework, and control gaps.
  * Support transition from fragmented or locally customized ways of working toward more structured, repeatable, transparent, and auditable processes.
  * Recommend practical improvements that balance business value, operational feasibility, control requirements, product priorities, cost, stakeholder readiness, and implementation complexity.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established continuous improvement capability across finance systems, reporting, product governance, operational support, and transformation workflows.

  * Prepare structured updates, governance packs, roadmap summaries, decision points, risk summaries, escalation notes, business cases, and stakeholder communications.

  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Signature strength. Strong stakeholder communication and leadership capability across finance, IT, product, reporting, governance, architecture, vendors, and support teams.

## 5. Business Analysis, Requirements & Solution Shaping

* **Requirement Elicitation & Elaboration**
  * Engage with business, finance, IT, reporting, governance, architecture, security, access, support, and vendor stakeholders to clarify needs, current issues, target outcomes, constraints, dependencies, and acceptance expectations.
  * Convert unstructured input from meetings, emails, process discussions, governance topics, operational examples, system outputs, and stakeholder feedback into structured requirements.
  * Distinguish between business requirements, product requirements, process requirements, system requirements, reporting requirements, data needs, access requirements, control requirements, and governance expectations.
  * Ask clarifying questions to uncover hidden assumptions, edge cases, decision points, ownership gaps, access implications, integration dependencies, data-flow issues, and operational risks.
  * Support requirement alignment by helping stakeholders develop shared understanding of scope, impact, dependencies, priorities, and acceptance criteria.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established capability in eliciting, clarifying, structuring, validating, and governing requirements across finance systems, product, reporting, governance, and transformation contexts.

* **Requirement Lifecycle and Delivery Traceability**
  * Link requirements to product backlog items, user stories, epics, risks, change requests, test activities, acceptance criteria, governance actions, and support-model impacts.
  * Use Jira and Confluence to manage requirements, epics, user stories, tasks, testing, project notes, decisions, documentation, and delivery tracking.
  * Support requirement validation by checking whether proposed solutions address the original business need and whether assumptions, constraints, and impacts are clear.
  * Contribute to traceability from initial need through impact assessment, backlog prioritization, delivery planning, testing, approval, release, acceptance, and post-change follow-up.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established requirement lifecycle and delivery traceability capability, supported by hands-on Jira and Confluence usage.

* **Solution Design Support**
  * Translate business needs into high-level solution concepts, including process changes, data requirements, reporting needs, access requirements, governance steps, user interactions, support processes, and automation logic.
  * Bridge business and technical perspectives by explaining what the process needs to achieve, what dependencies exist, what controls matter, and what constraints must be considered.
  * Work with enterprise, data, architecture, reporting, infrastructure, and development teams on solution design, integration design, architecture awareness, system-state documentation, and target-state discussions.
  * Avoid overstating ownership of detailed technical architecture or platform-specific configuration unless directly supported by examples.
  * **Capability Level**: Established / Developing.
  * **Positioning**: Strong business-side and product-side solution design support capability, especially in translating process, reporting, access, governance, and finance-system needs into structured solution inputs.

## 6. Process Excellence, Process Architecture & Documentation

* **Current-State Process Discovery**
  * Analyze existing business, finance, reporting, technology, governance, access, compliance, and support processes using stakeholder interviews, operational walkthroughs, system outputs, documentation, meetings, reports, tickets, and working artefacts.
  * Reconstruct undocumented or partially documented processes by identifying actors, inputs, outputs, systems, handoffs, approvals, controls, exceptions, risks, decision points, and pain points.

  * Document how processes actually work in practice, including informal workarounds, escalation paths, decision points, acceptance steps, evidence requirements, and closure routines.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Signature strength. Strong capability in discovering, reconstructing, and documenting current-state processes across finance, reporting, product, governance, access, and support environments.

* **Process Mapping and Process Architecture**
  * Create and maintain process maps, workflow diagrams, swimlane diagrams, decision trees, responsibility views, onboarding plans, governance flows, support-model documentation, and structured process narratives.
  * Use Visio extensively to create process maps, architecture diagrams, integration views, data-flow views, governance diagrams, and target-state process documentation.
  * Create process documentation that supports stakeholder alignment, onboarding, governance, auditability, change assessment, product delivery, support handover, and operating model improvement.
  * Understand BPMN 2.0 and use BPMN-style thinking; comfortable using Camunda, Bizagi, and Lucidchart based on training/free-trial use, while Visio remains the primary proven tool.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established process mapping and process architecture capability, with extensive Visio experience and comfortable BPMN/modelling-tool familiarity.

* **Process Standardization & Design**
  * Identify opportunities for process standardization, template harmonization, governance clarification, automation, simplification, ownership definition, control improvement, and support-model maturity.
  * Compare process variants and identify where more consistent practices could reduce complexity, improve accountability, reduce risk, and support governance.
  * Recommend process design improvements that balance standardization with legitimate business, market, reporting, system, support, or control requirements.
  * Support transition from manual, fragmented, or unclear workflows toward more structured, repeatable, transparent, and auditable processes.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established process standardization and design capability across finance, reporting, governance, support, and technology product workflows.

* **Documentation and Knowledge Management**
  * Create and maintain structured documentation, knowledge pages, requirements pages, decision logs, process documentation, support materials, governance notes, onboarding documentation, and project records.
  * Use Confluence, SharePoint, PowerPoint, Excel, Jira, and Visio to maintain product, process, delivery, support, and governance knowledge.
  * Support auditability, traceability, onboarding, knowledge retention, and delivery transparency through structured documentation.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established documentation and knowledge-management capability across product, process, governance, and delivery contexts.
