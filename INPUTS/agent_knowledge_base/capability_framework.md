# Enterprise Capability Framework

This framework represents a realistic, evidence-based competency profile for a Business Process Analyst, Transformation Analyst, Finance Systems Analyst, Product / Technology Management contributor, and developing Business Architect profile.

It is intended for CV positioning, role matching, interview preparation, capability mapping, learning planning, and skill development.

The framework distinguishes between:

* **Established capabilities**: areas supported by repeated professional application, current-role evidence, or credible enterprise delivery exposure.
* **Established / Developing capabilities**: areas where there is real professional evidence, but where ownership should still be framed carefully.
* **Developing capabilities**: areas currently being strengthened through structured learning, hands-on projects, role-adjacent exposure, or partial professional involvement.
* **Awareness-level capabilities**: areas where conceptual understanding exists, but which should not yet be presented as direct ownership or specialist expertise unless supported by specific examples.

## 1. Strategy & Transformation

* **Transformation Analysis & Roadmap Support**
  * Analyze current-state business processes, system dependencies, delivery risks, operational gaps, and stakeholder concerns to support transformation planning.
  * Structure transformation topics into clear problem statements, target directions, dependencies, risks, milestones, and proposed next steps.
  * Support roadmap discussions by clarifying what needs to change, why it matters, what dependencies exist, and what decisions are required.
  * Contribute to migration-path, roadmap, impact, benefit, and milestone discussions across finance systems, reporting, governance, and product / technology management contexts.
  * Translate complex enterprise change topics into structured updates that support decision-making, prioritization, and stakeholder alignment.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established capability in transformation analysis and roadmap support, particularly in finance systems, reporting, governance, and enterprise change contexts.

* **Operating Model & Ways-of-Working Improvement**
  * Analyze how governance routines, ownership models, decision rights, communication channels, approval flows, and support processes influence operational effectiveness.
  * Identify process friction caused by unclear ownership, fragmented communication, inconsistent prioritization, manual approvals, unclear severity handling, or missing end-to-end governance.
  * Support definition of improved ways of working, including clearer triage, backlog handling, assessment steps, acceptance criteria, escalation routes, and closure routines.
  * Contribute to operating model change discussions by clarifying roles, handoffs, hypercare needs, continuity arrangements, and service-delivery implications.
  * **Capability Level**: Established / Developing.
  * **Positioning**: Strong practical capability in improving ways of working and governance routines, with developing maturity toward broader operating model design.

* **Financial and Business Case Awareness**
  * Assess transformation and reporting-change topics using cost, effort, stability, dependency, capability, and operational-risk considerations.
  * Support cost-benefit thinking by identifying third-party costs, internal staffing implications, manual effort, rework, process inefficiencies, and dependency risks.
  * Contribute to cost planning and benefit framing by structuring business rationale, expected impact, budget implications, and review points.
  * Understand how cost, benefit, capacity, risk, and delivery feasibility influence transformation prioritization.
  * Avoid claiming full ownership of enterprise CAPEX/OPEX governance or investment portfolio management unless directly evidenced.
  * **Capability Level**: Established / Developing.
  * **Positioning**: Credible capability in practical business-case and cost-benefit analysis for transformation, reporting, and finance-systems change topics.

* **Continuous Improvement & Operational Excellence**
  * Identify opportunities to simplify, standardize, document, and improve recurring business processes.
  * Analyze manual, spreadsheet-heavy, approval-driven, governance-heavy, and cross-functional workflows to identify inefficiencies and control risks.
  * Support transition from fragmented or locally customized ways of working toward more structured, repeatable, transparent, and auditable processes.
  * Recommend practical improvements that balance business value, operational feasibility, governance needs, and stakeholder readiness.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established capability in identifying practical improvement opportunities across finance systems, reporting, governance, and operational workflows.

## 2. Business Architecture

* **Business Capability Awareness & Mapping**
  * Understand the purpose of business capability models, value streams, process hierarchies, application landscapes, and technology rationalization.
  * Participate in discussions that connect business capabilities, finance processes, system landscapes, data flows, reporting dependencies, and governance requirements.
  * Contribute business/process knowledge to architecture and capability discussions by explaining how work is performed, what systems support it, and where dependencies or risks exist.
  * Developing ability to connect processes, systems, data, ownership, controls, and strategic objectives into a structured business architecture view.
  * Avoid claiming ownership of enterprise capability maps, SAP LeanIX repositories, or enterprise architecture governance unless directly evidenced.
  * **Capability Level**: Developing / Awareness.
  * **Positioning**: Developing business architecture awareness supported by real participation in capability, architecture-scope, data-flow, and system-state discussions.

* **Requirement Elicitation & Elaboration**
  * Engage with business, finance, IT, reporting, governance, and architecture stakeholders to clarify needs, current issues, target outcomes, constraints, and dependencies.
  * Convert unstructured input from meetings, emails, process discussions, governance topics, operational examples, and stakeholder feedback into structured requirements.
  * Distinguish between business requirements, process requirements, system requirements, reporting requirements, data needs, control requirements, and governance expectations.
  * Ask clarifying questions to uncover hidden assumptions, edge cases, decision points, ownership gaps, access implications, and operational risks.
  * Support requirement alignment by helping stakeholders develop a shared understanding of scope, impact, dependencies, and acceptance expectations.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established capability in eliciting, clarifying, structuring, and validating requirements across finance systems, reporting, governance, and transformation contexts.

* **Business Architecture Repository & Documentation Awareness**
  * Understand the value of maintaining structured documentation for business processes, capabilities, systems, interfaces, decisions, controls, and ownership.
  * Contribute process, requirement, governance, and system-context information into structured documents, repositories, decks, trackers, and knowledge artefacts.
  * Support documentation quality by clarifying context, assumptions, dependencies, ownership, decisions, risks, and evidence.
  * Familiar with the concept of architecture repositories and structured knowledge bases, but should not claim repository ownership unless directly evidenced.
  * **Capability Level**: Developing / Awareness.
  * **Positioning**: Able to contribute structured process, requirement, system-context, and governance documentation to support enterprise knowledge management.

## 3. Process Excellence & Automation

* **Current-State Process Discovery**
  * Analyze existing business processes using stakeholder interviews, operational walkthroughs, emails, reports, system outputs, governance discussions, documentation, meetings, and working artefacts.
  * Reconstruct undocumented or partially documented processes by identifying actors, inputs, outputs, handoffs, approvals, systems, exceptions, controls, risks, and pain points.
  * Identify manual work, duplicate effort, local process variations, spreadsheet dependency, rework loops, unclear ownership, missing controls, and governance bottlenecks.
  * Document how processes actually work in practice, including informal workarounds, escalation paths, decision points, acceptance steps, and closure routines.
  * Apply current-state discovery to finance systems, reporting, governance, access, hierarchy, and change-management contexts.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Signature strength. Strong established capability in discovering, reconstructing, and documenting current-state processes, including manual workarounds, system touchpoints, stakeholder handoffs, and governance gaps.

* **Process Modeling & Structured Documentation**
  * Create process maps, workflow descriptions, structured process narratives, decision trees, responsibility views, onboarding plans, governance flows, and step-by-step documentation.
  * Document process steps, decision points, loops, handoffs, acceptance criteria, exceptions, risks, and dependencies in a clear and structured way.
  * Prepare structured documentation that supports stakeholder alignment, onboarding, governance, change assessment, and delivery planning.
  * Understand BPMN 2.0 concepts and process hierarchy principles, with formal BPMN notation still developing.
  * Able to document finance and operational processes involving SAP, BISON, NEXUS, SIERA, SAC, Power BI, Excel, approvals, access, governance meetings, and cross-functional coordination.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established capability in process modelling and structured documentation, with formal BPMN notation as a developing specialist skill.

* **Process Standardization & Design**
  * Identify opportunities for process standardization, template harmonization, governance clarification, automation, simplification, ownership definition, and control improvement.
  * Compare process variants and identify where more consistent practices could reduce complexity, improve accountability, and support governance.
  * Recommend practical process design improvements that balance standardization with legitimate business, market, reporting, system, or control requirements.
  * Support transition from highly manual, fragmented, or unclear workflows toward more structured, repeatable, transparent, and auditable processes.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established capability in identifying process standardization and simplification opportunities across finance systems, reporting, governance, and operational workflows.

* **Automation Opportunity Assessment**
  * Identify candidate tasks for automation, especially repetitive data handling, reporting preparation, validation, routing, documentation, access coordination, tracking, and status-reporting activities.
  * Assess whether automation is appropriate based on task frequency, rules clarity, data quality, process stability, control requirements, business risk, and stakeholder readiness.
  * Translate automation opportunities into process logic, input/output definitions, business rules, user interactions, and practical implementation considerations.
  * Professional evidence supports automation-opportunity thinking; hands-on technical automation delivery should be positioned separately as developing personal capability.
  * **Capability Level**: Established / Developing.
  * **Positioning**: Strong capability in identifying and structuring automation opportunities from a business/process perspective, with hands-on technical automation still developing.

* **Process Mining and Data-Driven Discovery Awareness**
  * Understand the concept of using event logs, system records, workflow data, and operational timestamps to analyze process performance and bottlenecks.
  * Aware of process mining as a method for discovering real process flows, variants, delays, rework, and deviations.
  * Able to see where data-driven discovery could support process analysis, but should not claim process mining implementation unless direct tool or project experience exists.
  * **Capability Level**: Awareness.
  * **Positioning**: Aware of process mining and data-driven discovery concepts, with potential to apply them in future process improvement work.

## 4. Product & Delivery Management

* **Product-Oriented Business Analysis**
  * Apply product-oriented thinking to internal business, reporting, finance systems, and technology-management problems by focusing on user needs, business value, scope, prioritization, risk, and incremental delivery.
  * Translate business problems into structured features, requirements, delivery items, acceptance expectations, and stakeholder actions.
  * Support roadmap, scope, prioritization, migration, and delivery-alignment discussions.
  * Understand the connection between process pain points, user experience, reporting outcomes, system functionality, access requirements, and measurable business impact.
  * Avoid claiming full product strategy ownership or end-to-end product lifecycle accountability unless directly evidenced.
  * **Capability Level**: Developing / Credible.
  * **Positioning**: Credible product-oriented business analysis and coordination capability, particularly for internal finance/reporting systems and transformation topics; not positioned as full product ownership unless supported by specific evidence.

* **Agile Product Delivery Awareness**
  * Understand agile delivery concepts such as backlog refinement, user stories, sprint planning, acceptance criteria, testing, defect triage, delivery tracking, and iterative feedback.
  * Able to collaborate with delivery and testing teams by clarifying requirements, validating business logic, supporting impact assessment, and aligning on expected outcomes.
  * Exposure to planning, testing, and defect-triage environments exists, but formal ownership of agile ceremonies, backlog management, or sprint delivery should be claimed only if directly evidenced.
  * **Capability Level**: Developing / Awareness.
  * **Positioning**: Familiar with agile and delivery concepts and able to support requirement clarification, business validation, testing alignment, and delivery coordination.

* **Requirement Lifecycle Support**
  * Document requirements and link them to process issues, stakeholder needs, expected outcomes, solution options, change impacts, and validation criteria.
  * Support requirement validation by checking whether proposed solutions address the original business need and whether assumptions are clear.
  * Contribute to traceability from initial need through impact assessment, delivery discussion, testing, acceptance, and post-change follow-up.
  * Developing stronger capability in full end-to-end requirement lifecycle governance across formal delivery frameworks.
  * **Capability Level**: Established / Developing.
  * **Positioning**: Credible capability in requirement lifecycle support, especially requirement clarification, documentation, impact assessment, validation, and stakeholder alignment.

* **Solution Design Support**
  * Translate business needs into high-level solution concepts, including process changes, data requirements, reporting needs, access requirements, governance steps, user interactions, and automation logic.
  * Bridge business and technical perspectives by explaining what the process needs to achieve, what dependencies exist, what controls matter, and what constraints must be considered.
  * Support architecture, data-flow, reporting, and system-state discussions from a business/process perspective.
  * Avoid overstating ownership of detailed technical architecture, solution architecture, or system implementation unless directly evidenced.
  * **Capability Level**: Established / Developing.
  * **Positioning**: Strong business-side solution design support capability, especially in translating process, reporting, access, and governance needs into structured requirements and decision inputs.

## 5. Enterprise Architecture & Technology

* **Enterprise Application & Integration Awareness**
  * Understand that business processes are supported by application landscapes, reporting tools, databases, integrations, manual extracts, access workflows, and governance platforms.
  * Identify where operational workflows depend on SAP, BISON, NEXUS, SIERA, SAC, Power BI, Excel, access tools, documentation repositories, approval flows, and support processes.
  * Participate in architecture-scope, data-flow, access-management, hierarchy, and system-state discussions from a business/process perspective.
  * Developing ability to connect business processes with applications, data flows, ownership, controls, and technology dependencies.
  * Avoid claiming independent ownership of enterprise architecture, integration architecture, or application portfolio governance unless directly evidenced.
  * **Capability Level**: Developing / Awareness.
  * **Positioning**: More than theoretical familiarity: developing enterprise architecture awareness supported by participation in application, reporting, hierarchy, access, and data-flow discussions.

* **Finance Systems and SAP Context**
  * Analyze finance systems workflows involving SAP, SAP ECC reporting context, BISON, NEXUS, SIERA, SAC, Power BI, Excel, approvals, governance routines, and reporting dependencies.
  * Understand how SAP outputs, reporting tools, finance data, hierarchy changes, access models, and downstream reporting requirements interact.
  * Support impact assessment for finance reporting changes, SAP dependencies, hierarchy changes, management reporting, access, and governance requirements.
  * Identify risks and dependencies caused by fragmented system use, unclear ownership, inconsistent processes, manual data handling, and reporting landscape complexity.
  * SAP experience should be positioned around business-side process, reporting, governance, and impact analysis unless direct configuration/development ownership exists.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established finance systems and SAP process-context analysis capability, especially around reporting dependencies, governance, hierarchy, access, and change impact.

* **Legacy Systems and Reverse Engineering Awareness**
  * Analyze existing workflows, reports, spreadsheets, governance routines, access models, and system outputs to understand how business logic and dependencies currently operate.
  * Support reverse engineering of undocumented process logic by reviewing operational files, reports, email threads, meeting notes, system references, and stakeholder explanations.
  * Identify gaps between intended process design and actual operational practice.
  * Developing technical understanding of databases, applications, code, and system structures through hands-on learning projects.
  * Avoid claiming deep codebase reverse engineering, legacy migration architecture, or technical modernization ownership unless supported by direct evidence.
  * **Capability Level**: Developing / Awareness.
  * **Positioning**: Able to support reverse engineering of business processes, reporting dependencies, and operational logic through structured analysis of artefacts and stakeholder input.

* **Cloud Infrastructure and Integration Awareness**
  * Understand high-level cloud, API, integration, hosting, identity, and reporting-architecture concepts.
  * Awareness of Azure and API management concepts through learning and enterprise-context exposure.
  * Can discuss cloud and integration topics at a conceptual level where they affect reporting landscapes, application dependencies, and modernization discussions.
  * Should not claim cloud architecture ownership, infrastructure design, or production Azure implementation unless directly supported by professional evidence.
  * **Capability Level**: Awareness / Developing.
  * **Positioning**: Developing awareness of cloud and integration concepts relevant to reporting, application landscapes, API management, and enterprise modernization.

* **Technical Architecture Awareness**
  * Understand high-level concepts around systems, databases, APIs, integrations, identity, access, cloud platforms, and application architecture.
  * Able to discuss technical dependencies with IT stakeholders and identify where business processes rely on systems, reports, data extracts, integrations, or access controls.
  * Developing hands-on technical capability through Python, SQLite, SQLAlchemy, OpenAI API integration, and modular desktop application development outside core Haleon evidence.
  * Avoid presenting as a technical architect, solution architect, integration architect, security architect, or senior software engineer unless backed by formal delivery experience.
  * **Capability Level**: Developing.
  * **Positioning**: Builds practical technical awareness through hands-on learning and prototypes, enabling stronger collaboration between business and IT teams.

## 6. Stakeholder & Governance Management

* **Stakeholder Engagement & Communication**
  * Communicate clearly with business, finance, IT, reporting, architecture, governance, support, and leadership stakeholders.
  * Prepare structured updates, meeting inputs, risk summaries, roadmap notes, follow-up emails, decision points, and clarification questions.
  * Translate complex process, system, reporting, access, governance, and transformation topics into clear messages for different stakeholder groups.
  * Support alignment by clarifying risks, dependencies, options, required decisions, expected benefits, next steps, and ownership.
  * Manage sensitive communication carefully, including clarification of intent, escalation of risks, and constructive stakeholder engagement.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Signature strength. Strong established stakeholder communication capability across finance, IT, reporting, governance, architecture, and transformation contexts.

* **Governance and Control Awareness**
  * Analyze governance processes involving access management, approvals, SDLC controls, SOX considerations, documentation, evidence, rationale, ownership, and change impact.
  * Identify where unclear ownership, incomplete documentation, manual approvals, missing evidence, or fragmented processes create governance and control risks.
  * Support governance discussions by clarifying roles, access processes, provisioning, gaps, responsibilities, control implications, and required documentation.
  * Participate in governance and control-related discussions involving SAC access, hierarchy changes, GRC roles, mappings, job objects, questionnaires, and evidence requirements.
  * Avoid claiming specialist GRC, identity-governance, access-control, or security-tool ownership unless directly evidenced.
  * **Capability Level**: Established / Developing.
  * **Positioning**: Strong practical governance and control-awareness capability, particularly around access, approvals, documentation, SDLC/SOX considerations, and process evidence.

* **Enterprise IT Service & Change Governance Awareness**
  * Understand that enterprise IT changes require structured intake, prioritization, impact assessment, approval, testing, deployment, communication, acceptance, and closure.
  * Analyze change governance steps including triage, backlog handling, assessment, statement-of-work initiation, ServiceNow change creation, acceptance, closure, and support handover.
  * Support business-side clarification of change impact, dependencies, required approvals, testing expectations, stakeholder actions, and governance documentation.
  * Should not claim ownership of ServiceNow, SAP ChaRM, SolMan, release management, or ITSM administration unless directly supported by role evidence.
  * **Capability Level**: Established / Developing.
  * **Positioning**: Strong business-side understanding of IT service and change governance, with ability to document and improve change-related workflows.

* **Vendor, Resource and Portfolio Awareness**
  * Understand that transformation and technology initiatives often require vendor coordination, SOW handling, resource planning, dependency management, prioritization, and delivery governance.
  * Practical exposure to Fieldglass/SOW-related processes and external workforce coordination.
  * Contribute to discussions about capacity constraints, delivery risks, dependencies, ownership, and business priorities.
  * Should not claim independent ownership of RFPs, vendor bid evaluation, SLA management, Fieldglass administration, or enterprise portfolio management unless directly evidenced.
  * **Capability Level**: Awareness / Developing.
  * **Positioning**: Practical awareness of vendor, resource, and SOW-related governance, with ability to support dependency and risk discussions.

* **Controlled Document and Repository Governance Awareness**
  * Understand the importance of documentation ownership, review cycles, version control, auditability, evidence, approval trails, and knowledge retention.
  * Create and structure process documentation, requirement notes, meeting summaries, decision records, governance inputs, onboarding plans, and knowledge-base-style content.
  * Familiar with controlled documentation and repository concepts through enterprise documentation and governance contexts.
  * Should not claim ownership of regulated document platforms such as Veeva Vault unless directly evidenced.
  * **Capability Level**: Established / Awareness.
  * **Positioning**: Strong practical documentation capability with awareness of versioning, ownership, evidence, review, and auditability needs.

## 7. Data, Reporting & Analytics

  * Analyze business data, reports, templates, hierarchy information, reporting inputs, operational assumptions, and finance-system outputs using structured logic.
  * Understand spreadsheet-heavy business processes, including their strengths, limitations, manual effort, control risks, and data-quality challenges.
  * Identify opportunities to improve reporting consistency, reduce manual manipulation, simplify reporting workflows, and clarify ownership.
  * Support business decision-making by structuring information, summarizing findings, and explaining process, data, or reporting issues.
  * **Capability Level**: Established.
  * **Positioning**: Established practical capability in Excel-based reporting, operational analysis, finance-system outputs, and reporting-process improvement.

* **BI, KPI and Reporting Requirements**
  * Define and clarify reporting requirements, scope, hierarchy dependencies, source-system impacts, ownership expectations, and stakeholder needs.
  * Contribute to reporting and BI governance discussions across SAC, Power BI, SAP, BISON, NEXUS, SIERA, and external reporting contexts.
  * Understand the role of dashboards, KPIs, reporting models, management reporting, hierarchy structures, and data visualization in business decision-making.
  * Evidence supports business-side requirements, governance, scope, and landscape coordination more strongly than hands-on BI model building.
  * Avoid claiming advanced Power BI development, SAC model ownership, DAX, or enterprise BI engineering unless directly evidenced.
  * **Capability Level**: Developing / Awareness.
  * **Positioning**: Credible business-side BI and reporting capability focused on requirements, scope, dependencies, governance, and impact rather than hands-on dashboard/model development.

* **SQL and Relational Data Handling**
  * Developing practical capability in querying, structuring, and managing relational data using SQL and database concepts.
  * Understand tables, relationships, filtering, joins, basic schema thinking, and how structured data supports applications and reporting.
  * Uses database concepts in personal and learning projects, including SQLite and SQLAlchemy-based application development.
