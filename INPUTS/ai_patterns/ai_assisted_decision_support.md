# AI-Assisted Decision Support Pattern

## Purpose
Use AI to support better, faster, evidence-based decisions by synthesizing information, comparing scenarios, prioritizing risks, and explaining trade-offs.

## When to Use
Use this pattern when decisions require multiple data sources, uncertain evidence, recurring judgment, business trade-offs, or documented rationale.

## Typical Pain Points Addressed
- limited_real_time_insights
- zombie_data
- data_fragmentation
- poor_master_data_management
- manual_reconciliation
- fragmented_governance

## Business Capabilities Supported
- management reporting
- risk management
- financial planning
- operational planning
- enterprise architecture management
- portfolio management
- governance management

## Technology Capabilities Required
- data integration
- analytics and semantic models
- retrieval-augmented generation
- scenario modeling
- rules and decision management
- visualization
- audit logging

## Typical Use Cases
- management insights
- forecasting support
- risk prioritization
- scenario comparison
- exception analysis
- operational recommendations
- architecture option assessment

## Operating Model Impact
- shifts analysis from manual evidence gathering to guided decision review
- improves consistency of decision rationale
- requires clear decision rights
- requires human approval before material decisions
- creates reusable decision records

## Data Requirements
- trusted data sources
- metric definitions
- historical performance data
- business rules
- risk criteria
- evidence sources
- assumptions and confidence ratings

## Integration Requirements
- data warehouse or lakehouse
- BI platforms
- ERP and operational systems
- planning systems
- risk and GRC systems
- knowledge repositories
- workflow approval tools

## Governance and Risk Considerations
- separate evidence from inference
- document assumptions and confidence
- avoid black-box recommendations
- require human approval for material decisions
- monitor data freshness and model quality
- maintain decision audit trail

## Value Drivers
- faster decision cycles
- better evidence quality
- reduced analysis effort
- improved decision consistency
- clearer audit trail
- improved prioritization

## Success Metrics
- decision cycle time
- analysis effort reduction
- forecast accuracy
- number of decisions with documented rationale
- stakeholder satisfaction
- reduced rework from poor decisions

## Common Failure Modes
- weak source data
- unclear decision ownership
- overreliance on AI recommendations
- insufficient explainability
- stale data
- recommendations not embedded into workflow
