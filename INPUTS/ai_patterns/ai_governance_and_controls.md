# AI Governance and Controls Pattern

## Purpose
Define governance, risk, compliance, and control patterns for safe, scalable, and auditable AI adoption.

## When to Use
Use this pattern when AI is used in regulated, customer-facing, financial, operational, decision-support, or external-facing contexts.

## Typical Use Cases
- AI usage policy
- human-in-the-loop controls
- AI use case intake and prioritization

- model risk management
- AI solution lifecycle governance
- approval workflows for external outputs

## Operating Model Impact
- introduces accountable AI ownership
- creates review gates for high-risk use cases
- aligns architecture, data, security, risk, and business stakeholders
- moves AI adoption from experimentation to governed scaling
- improves trust in AI-enabled transformation

## Integration Requirements
- GRC platforms
- ITSM platforms
- model registry or AI platform
- identity provider
- data catalog
- security monitoring
- workflow approval tools
- audit evidence repositories

## Governance and Risk Considerations
- clear accountable owner for each AI use case
- risk-based review gates
- data privacy and security controls
- explainability requirements for high-impact decisions
- human approval for external or material outputs
- continuous monitoring for quality, bias, drift, and misuse
- controls for shadow AI usage

## Value Drivers
- faster safe AI adoption
- reduced compliance exposure
- better stakeholder trust
- scalable control model
- improved audit readiness
- reduced unmanaged AI risk

## Success Metrics
- percentage of AI use cases risk-assessed
- approval cycle time
- number of AI incidents or control breaches
- audit findings reduction
- policy compliance rate
- monitored AI use cases
- stakeholder trust score

## Common Failure Modes
- governance added too late
- unclear ownership
- excessive bureaucracy blocking adoption
- no monitoring after deployment
- unmanaged shadow AI
- weak data access controls
- lack of explainability
