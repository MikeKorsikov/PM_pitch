# Finance Industry Pattern

## Industry Characteristics

* Highly regulated, control-driven operating model
* Data-intensive transaction processing, reporting, and reconciliation
* Strong dependency on month-end, quarter-end, and year-end cycles
* High sensitivity to auditability, risk, fraud, and segregation of duties
* Complex legal entity, cost center, product, and account structures
* Significant legacy platform dependency across ERP, risk, treasury, and reporting
* Transformation pressure from cost optimization, real-time insight, and regulatory change

---

## Core Business Capabilities

* Financial Planning & Analysis
* Record to Report
* Close & Consolidation
* Treasury Management
* Risk Management
* Regulatory Reporting
* Tax Management
* Internal Controls Management
* Master Data Governance
* Management Reporting

---

## Common Enterprise Platforms

* ERP: SAP ECC, SAP S/4HANA, Oracle ERP Cloud, Microsoft Dynamics
* EPM / FP&A: SAP Analytics Cloud, Oracle EPM, Anaplan, Workday Adaptive Planning
* Reconciliation / Close: BlackLine, Trintech
* Risk / GRC: SAP GRC, Archer, ServiceNow GRC
* Data / Analytics: Snowflake, Databricks, Power BI, Tableau
* ITSM / Workflow: ServiceNow, Jira Service Management

---

## Typical Pain Points

* Manual reconciliation
* Excel middleware
* Fragmented governance
* Duplicated reporting logic
* Poor E2E visibility
* Slow close cycles
* Shadow IT analytics
* Poor master data management
* Legacy system dependency
* Integration complexity

---

## Common Architecture Characteristics

* ERP-centric core with satellite finance applications
* Heavy batch processing and scheduled data extracts
* Multiple data marts and reporting layers
* High spreadsheet dependency for planning, controls, and reconciliations
* Point-to-point integrations between ERP, banks, tax, risk, and reporting systems
* Acquisition-driven process and chart-of-account fragmentation

---

## Data Characteristics

* High-volume financial transaction data
* Complex chart of accounts, entity, product, and cost center hierarchies
* Strict auditability, lineage, retention, and reconciliation requirements
* Material dependency on reference and master data quality
* Strong demand for near-real-time cash, risk, and performance visibility
* Reporting logic often duplicated across spreadsheets, BI, and EPM tools

---

## Governance & Regulatory Constraints

* SOX and internal financial controls
* Segregation of duties and privileged access controls
* Audit trail, evidence management, and financial traceability
* Data retention and recordkeeping requirements
* GDPR and regional data privacy obligations
* Model risk governance for AI-enabled forecasting or decision support
* Cybersecurity controls for payment, treasury, and financial data

---
