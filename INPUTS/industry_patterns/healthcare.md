# Healthcare Industry Pattern

## Industry Characteristics

* Patient-, safety-, and outcome-driven operating model
* High regulatory intensity and strict privacy requirements
* Complex clinical, administrative, financial, and operational workflows
* Strong dependency on interoperability across providers, payers, labs, pharmacies, and devices
* High variation across facilities, specialties, and care pathways
* Workforce capacity constraints and high operational pressure
* Transformation pressure from patient access, cost control, care quality, and digital health adoption

---

* Patient Access Management
* Care Delivery
* Clinical Documentation
* Care Coordination
* Revenue Cycle Management
* Claims & Billing
* Population Health Management
* Quality & Safety Management
* Clinical Operations Management
* Supply & Pharmacy Management
* Regulatory Reporting
* Master Data Governance

---

## Common Enterprise Platforms

* EHR / EMR: Epic, Oracle Health, MEDITECH, Allscripts
* ERP / Finance: Workday, Oracle ERP, SAP S/4HANA
* Revenue Cycle: Epic, Oracle Health, Waystar, R1
* CRM / Patient Engagement: Salesforce Health Cloud, Microsoft Cloud for Healthcare
* Integration / Interoperability: HL7, FHIR platforms, Mirth, InterSystems
* Data / Analytics: Snowflake, Databricks, Power BI, Tableau
* ITSM / Workflow: ServiceNow, Jira Service Management
* Imaging / Clinical Systems: PACS, LIS, RIS, pharmacy systems

---

## Typical Pain Points

* Poor E2E visibility
* Data fragmentation
* Inefficient workflow
* Legacy system dependency
* Integration complexity
* Poor customer experience
* Manual reconciliation
* Compliance audit complexity
* Knowledge risk
* Limited real-time insights

---

## Common Architecture Characteristics

* EHR-centric landscape with many specialized clinical and administrative systems
* Complex interoperability patterns using HL7, FHIR, APIs, files, and interface engines
* Department-specific applications and workflow variation
* Heavy dependency on identity, consent, scheduling, billing, and clinical documentation integration
* Cloud adoption balanced against privacy, security, and clinical resilience requirements
* Data platforms used for quality reporting, population health, operations, and finance

---

## Data Characteristics

* Sensitive patient, clinical, claims, billing, provider, and operational data
* High need for longitudinal patient records and cross-system identity resolution
* Complex interoperability, terminology, and coding standards
* Strong retention, consent, auditability, and access control requirements
* Real-time needs for patient flow, bed capacity, care coordination, and safety alerts
* Data quality issues from fragmented systems, free text, coding variation, and manual entry

---

## Governance & Regulatory Constraints

* HIPAA and regional health data privacy requirements
* GDPR where applicable
* Clinical safety and patient consent governance
* Auditability for access to protected health information
* Medical device and clinical system cybersecurity controls
* Billing, coding, and claims compliance
* Quality reporting and accreditation requirements
* AI governance for clinical decision support and patient-facing automation
