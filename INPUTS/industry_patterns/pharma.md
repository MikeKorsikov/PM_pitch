# Pharma Industry Pattern

## Industry Characteristics

* Highly regulated, quality-driven operating model
* Long product lifecycle from research to commercialization
* Strong dependency on validated processes, documentation, and audit evidence
* Complex manufacturing, quality, batch release, and supply chain controls
* High data lineage, traceability, and retention requirements
* Global regulatory variation across products, markets, and sites
* Transformation pressure from R&D productivity, supply resilience, and compliance modernization

---

## Core Business Capabilities

* Research & Development
* Clinical Operations
* Regulatory Affairs
* Quality Management
* Pharmacovigilance
* Manufacturing Operations
* Batch Release Management
* Supply Chain Planning
* Product Lifecycle Management
* Commercial Operations
* Master Data Governance

---

## Common Enterprise Platforms

* ERP: SAP ECC, SAP S/4HANA, Oracle ERP
* Quality: Veeva Quality, TrackWise, MasterControl
* Regulatory / RIM: Veeva Vault RIM, ArisGlobal
* Clinical: Medidata, Oracle Clinical, Veeva Clinical
* MES / Manufacturing: Werum PAS-X, Siemens Opcenter, Rockwell
* LIMS / Lab: LabWare, Thermo Fisher SampleManager
* PLM: Dassault, Siemens Teamcenter
* Data / Analytics: Snowflake, Databricks, Power BI, Tableau

---

## Typical Pain Points

* Compliance audit complexity
* Fragmented governance
* Manual documentation workflows
* Legacy system dependency
* Poor E2E visibility
* Poor master data management
* Integration complexity
* Slow change
* Tribal knowledge
* Data fragmentation

---

## Common Architecture Characteristics

* Validated application landscape with controlled change processes
* Site-specific manufacturing and quality systems
* ERP, MES, LIMS, QMS, RIM, and clinical systems integrated unevenly
* Heavy document-centric workflows and approval chains
* Regional and product-line variation in process and data models
* Long-lived legacy platforms due to validation and migration risk

---

## Data Characteristics

* High lineage and auditability requirements across product, batch, and quality data
* Complex product, material, recipe, supplier, site, and regulatory master data
* Strong retention requirements for clinical, quality, and manufacturing records
* Mixed structured and document-heavy data estate
* Limited real-time visibility across batch status, deviations, and supply risk
* Complex data governance due to GxP, privacy, and global regulatory needs

---

## Governance & Regulatory Constraints

* GxP validation requirements
* FDA, EMA, and regional health authority obligations
* 21 CFR Part 11 electronic records and signatures
* Data integrity expectations including ALCOA+
* Pharmacovigilance reporting obligations
* GDPR and health data privacy requirements
* Quality risk management and change control governance
* Computer system validation and audit evidence requirements

---
