# Retail Industry Pattern

## Industry Characteristics

* Customer- and margin-driven operating model
* High transaction volumes across stores, digital channels, marketplaces, and service channels
* Strong dependency on merchandising, pricing, inventory, fulfillment, and customer experience
* Demand volatility driven by seasonality, promotion, channel behavior, and macro conditions
* Complex supplier, product, location, and customer data ecosystems
* Thin-margin pressure requiring operational efficiency and rapid decision-making
* Transformation pressure from omnichannel expectations, personalization, and supply resilience

* ERP: SAP S/4HANA Retail, Oracle Retail, Microsoft Dynamics

* Data / Analytics: Snowflake, Databricks, Power BI, Tableau, Google BigQuery

* Integration complexity
* Shadow IT

* Manual reconciliation
* High operations costs

---

## Common Architecture Characteristics

* Omnichannel landscape spanning POS, ecommerce, OMS, WMS, CRM, and loyalty
* High integration load between digital, store, fulfillment, and analytics platforms
* Regional and banner-specific application fragmentation
* Legacy POS and merchandising systems alongside cloud commerce platforms
* Event-driven architecture emerging for inventory, order, and customer interactions
* Heavy dependency on data platforms for personalization, forecasting, and performance reporting

---

## Data Characteristics

* High-volume customer, product, transaction, inventory, and clickstream data
* Complex product, SKU, location, supplier, price, promotion, and customer master data
* Real-time needs for inventory availability, order status, pricing, and customer engagement
* Data quality gaps across channels and product hierarchies
* Privacy-sensitive customer and loyalty data
* Reporting fragmentation across merchandising, digital, stores, finance, and supply chain

---

## Governance & Regulatory Constraints

* GDPR, CCPA, and customer privacy obligations
* PCI DSS for payment processing
* Consumer protection, pricing, and promotion compliance
* Data consent and marketing preference governance
* Cybersecurity controls for ecommerce, POS, and customer data
* Product safety, labeling, and trade compliance where relevant
* Segregation of duties for finance, procurement, and pricing approvals
