# Poor Customer Experience

## Summary
Poor customer experience occurs when customers face inconsistent, slow, confusing, or low-quality interactions across channels, products, or service processes.

## Symptoms
- Customers repeat information across channels
- Service resolution is slow or inconsistent
- Digital and offline experiences are disconnected
- Customer complaints recur around the same process issues
- Frontline teams lack full customer or case context

## Typical Root Causes
- Fragmented customer data and channel systems
- Siloed ownership of customer journeys
- Manual handoffs and inefficient workflows
- Legacy systems limiting digital experience
- Poor service knowledge and inconsistent process execution

## Business Impact
- Lower customer satisfaction and loyalty
- Higher cost to serve
- Increased churn, complaints, or escalations
- Reduced revenue conversion
- Reputational and compliance risk in regulated interactions

## Architecture Implications
- Customer data is fragmented across CRM, service, billing, and operational systems
- Channels are integrated inconsistently
- Journey-level process visibility is weak
- Knowledge and case management are not embedded in service workflows
- Operating model lacks customer journey ownership

## Common Solution Directions
- Map customer journeys and pain points
- Integrate customer data across channels
- Improve case and knowledge management
- Automate routing and status updates
- Establish journey ownership and service standards

## Risks and Watchouts
- Optimizing channels without fixing end-to-end journeys
- Using AI chat without reliable knowledge sources
- Ignoring frontline employee workflow
- Overpersonalization without privacy controls
- Measuring activity rather than customer outcomes
