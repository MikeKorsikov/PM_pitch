# Shadow IT

## Summary
Shadow IT occurs when business teams create or buy technology outside formal IT, architecture, security, procurement, or data governance channels.

## Symptoms
- Teams rely on unmanaged SaaS tools, spreadsheets, scripts, or databases
- Critical processes run outside supported platforms
- IT discovers tools only during incidents, audits, or migrations
- Data is copied into local repositories without governance
- Similar tools are purchased by multiple teams

## Typical Root Causes
- Slow delivery from central IT
- Business urgency and unmet workflow needs
- Weak technology governance and procurement controls
- Lack of approved self-service platforms
- Poor communication between IT and business teams

## Business Impact
- Increased security, privacy, and compliance risk
- Duplicated licensing and support costs
- Fragmented data and process execution
- Higher operational dependency on local tool owners
- Slower enterprise transformation and rationalization

## Architecture Implications
- Application inventory is incomplete
- Data flows are uncontrolled or undocumented
- Integrations bypass standard patterns
- Architecture governance is reactive
- Operating model lacks safe channels for business innovation

- AI-assisted application and data flow discovery

- governance_maturity

## Typical KPIs and Evidence Signals
- number of unmanaged applications
- duplicate SaaS spend
- security exceptions from non-approved tools
- data extracts to local repositories
- architecture review bypass rate
- business satisfaction with approved platforms

## Common Solution Directions
- Establish transparent technology intake and review
- Provide approved low-code and automation platforms
- Rationalize duplicate tools
- Improve application portfolio visibility
- Balance governance with business self-service needs

## Risks and Watchouts
- Suppressing useful innovation without alternatives
- Missing critical local dependencies during cleanup
- Overly slow approval processes
- Ignoring change management for business-owned tools
- Failing to govern shadow AI usage
