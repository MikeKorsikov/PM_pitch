# Weak Architecture Standardisation

1. Symptoms
Multiple technology tools used to solve identical or overlapping business problems
Absence of standardized reference architectures and design guidelines
Inconsistent configuration and deployment patterns across systems
Architecture review processes bypassed or applied inconsistently
High variability in solution design across teams and regions
2. Root Causes
Decentralized IT procurement and budget ownership
Weak authority or enforcement capability of enterprise architecture governance
Lack of centralized reference architecture and reusable design patterns
Organizational prioritization of delivery speed over standardization
Limited architectural decision documentation and traceability
3. Business Impact
Increased software licensing and infrastructure costs due to duplication
Higher integration and maintenance overhead across systems
Increased security and compliance risks due to inconsistent designs
Slower modernization and platform migration efforts
Reduced scalability of enterprise technology landscape
4. Technology Indicators
Multiple overlapping systems serving the same business capability (e.g., multiple CRMs)
Inconsistent API design patterns and communication protocols
Diverse technology stacks without standardization guidelines
Fragmented database schemas and deployment patterns
Lack of shared libraries or reusable platform components
5. Architecture Implications
Technology sprawl and fragmented application landscape
Low reuse of services, components, and architectural assets
Increased complexity in infrastructure management and security enforcement
Reduced maintainability and higher technical debt accumulation
Weak alignment between business capabilities and system design
6. Common Solution Patterns
Establish enterprise architecture governance with formal review processes
Define and maintain enterprise reference architectures and design standards
Implement application portfolio rationalization frameworks
Standardize core technology stacks and integration patterns
Introduce architecture decision records (ADR) for traceability
Use enterprise architecture tools for cataloging and governance
7. AI & Automation Opportunities
AI-driven auditing of systems against reference architecture standards
Automated generation of architecture diagrams and system maps
Intelligent detection of technology stack duplication and redundancy
AI-assisted recommendation of standard architectural patterns
Automated compliance checking against architecture policies
8. KPIs / Metrics
Percentage of systems aligned to reference architecture standards
Application rationalization rate (systems decommissioned vs total)
Number of architecture policy exceptions
Technology stack reuse rate across domains
Reduction in software licensing and infrastructure costs
9. Risks of Transformation
Slowdown in delivery due to stricter architecture review processes
Resistance from teams losing autonomy in technology selection
High cost and effort required for system consolidation and migration
Temporary productivity loss during standardization initiatives
Risk of over-standardization reducing innovation flexibility
