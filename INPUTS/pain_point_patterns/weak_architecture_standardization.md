# Weak Architecture Standardization

## Summary
Weak architecture standardization occurs when technology design, platform choices, integration patterns, and delivery practices vary widely without clear enterprise standards.

## Symptoms
- Multiple tools solve the same business problem
- Teams use inconsistent design, integration, or deployment patterns
- Architecture review is bypassed or applied inconsistently
- Reference architectures are missing or outdated
- Technology exceptions are common and poorly governed

## Typical Root Causes
- Decentralized technology decision-making
- Weak enterprise architecture authority
- Delivery speed prioritized over long-term coherence
- Lack of reusable patterns and decision records
- Fragmented procurement and platform ownership

## Business Impact
- Higher technology cost and duplication
- Increased integration and support complexity
- Greater security, compliance, and operational risk
- Slower modernization and cloud migration
- Reduced reuse and scalability of enterprise platforms

## Architecture Implications
- Application and platform landscape becomes fragmented
- Services, APIs, and components are not reusable
- Security and operational controls vary by implementation
- Architecture decisions lack traceability
- Operating model struggles to enforce enterprise guardrails

## Common Solution Directions
- Define reference architectures and reusable patterns
- Strengthen architecture review and exception governance
- Maintain architecture decision records
- Rationalize duplicated platforms and technologies
- Connect standards to delivery tooling and procurement

## Risks and Watchouts
- Creating standards that slow delivery without adding value
- Over-standardization reducing necessary flexibility
- Weak enforcement by senior stakeholders
- Ignoring migration cost from existing variation
- Treating documentation as governance without adoption
