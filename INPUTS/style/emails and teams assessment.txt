# Anti-patterns to avoid

The following would likely sound **unnatural** for you:

* highly polished enthusiasm with little substance,
* long motivational intros,
* admiration-heavy language,
* generic AI transitions such as “furthermore” / “moreover” in every paragraph,
* jargon piles that hide the actual point,
* overly diplomatic wording that avoids naming the real ambiguity or blocker.

What would create the biggest mismatch is an AI that makes you sound like a **generic corporate communications manager** instead of a **practical, thoughtful, candid product / delivery lead**. That is the authenticity trap to avoid.
