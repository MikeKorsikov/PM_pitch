# AI Adoption Value Model

## Purpose
This value model helps justify responsible AI adoption and AI-enabled transformation by connecting AI use cases to productivity, decision quality, knowledge reuse, and operational intelligence.

## When to Use
Use this model when the case involves AI copilots, decision support, knowledge assistants, document intelligence, process intelligence, AI-assisted reporting, anomaly detection, or agentic workflow support.

## Primary Value Drivers
- productivity improvement
- faster decision cycles
- reduced knowledge search time
- better insight generation
- improved onboarding
- improved forecast quality
- reduced repetitive work
- improved service quality
- better operational intelligence

## Business Outcomes
- faster access to relevant knowledge
- improved quality and consistency of analysis
- reduced time spent on repetitive drafting or summarization
- improved decision support for managers and specialists
- better process visibility and exception handling
- higher adoption of standardized knowledge and workflows
- safer scaling of AI through governance and controls

## Typical Metrics and KPIs
- AI adoption rate
- active users
- usage frequency
- time saved per user
- decision cycle time
- knowledge retrieval time
- self-service resolution rate
- draft acceptance rate
- forecast accuracy
- exception detection rate
- user satisfaction

## Measurement Logic
AI adoption value can be estimated by combining adoption, relevance to the workflow, and measurable improvement in work outcomes.

Simple formulas:

- `productivity_gain = time_saved_per_user * number_of_users * adoption_rate`
- `decision_cycle_reduction = baseline_decision_time - target_decision_time`
- `knowledge_retrieval_savings = search_time_saved * frequency * user_population`
- `adoption_value = usage_rate * value_per_use_case`
- `ai_value_realization = adoption_rate * process_relevance * governance_maturity`

Avoid AI hype. Position AI as workforce augmentation, decision support, operational intelligence, and transformation acceleration.

Actual values require user or company validation.

## Typical Evidence Signals
- users spend significant time searching for knowledge
- reports or summaries are manually drafted
- decisions require repeated evidence gathering
- support teams answer repeated questions
- document-heavy workflows create bottlenecks
- operational exceptions are detected late
- AI pilots exist but value is not clearly measured

## Executive Framing
Position AI adoption as a governed capability for improving productivity, knowledge flow, decision support, and operational intelligence. Emphasize practical use cases, adoption, control, and measurable workflow impact rather than generic AI enthusiasm.

## Risks and Watchouts
- AI use cases not connected to real workflow pain points
- weak adoption despite strong pilots
- poor source grounding and hallucinated outputs
- unclear ownership of AI-enabled processes
- insufficient governance, security, or access controls
- overstated productivity claims without baseline measurement
- unmanaged shadow AI adoption
