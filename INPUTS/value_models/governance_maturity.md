# Governance Maturity Value Model

## Purpose
This value model helps justify governance improvements that increase decision clarity, reduce risk, improve compliance readiness, and enable scalable transformation.

## When to Use
Use this model when the case involves fragmented ownership, inconsistent processes, weak architecture standards, shadow IT, slow change, compliance complexity, poor control environment, or unclear decision rights.

## Primary Value Drivers
- better decision quality
- reduced operational risk
- improved compliance readiness
- faster change execution
- fewer architecture exceptions
- clearer ownership
- improved auditability
- reduced duplication
- stronger enterprise alignment

## Business Outcomes
- clearer accountability for decisions and outcomes
- fewer unmanaged exceptions
- reduced audit and compliance friction
- faster approval and change cycles
- improved standardization across teams or regions
- better investment prioritization
- stronger trust in architecture, data, and AI-enabled solutions

## Typical Metrics and KPIs
- governance maturity score
- decision cycle time
- number of unresolved ownership gaps
- architecture exception count
- policy compliance rate
- audit findings
- change success rate
- standardization coverage
- shadow IT reduction
- duplicated platform count

## Measurement Logic
Governance value is often evidenced through maturity improvement, risk reduction, and faster decision execution.

Simple formulas:

- `governance_maturity_score = weighted assessment of ownership, standards, controls, compliance, and decision rights`
- `risk_reduction_value = reduction in incidents, audit findings, or control failures`
- `standardization_coverage = standardized_processes / total_processes`
- `change_success_rate = successful_changes / total_changes`
- `exception_reduction = baseline_exceptions - target_exceptions`

Do not present governance as bureaucracy. Present governance as transformation enablement, scalability, control, and decision clarity.

Actual values require user or company validation.

## Executive Framing
Position governance maturity as a way to make transformation faster, safer, and more scalable. Emphasize decision clarity, reduced risk, audit readiness, and better enterprise alignment rather than additional control for its own sake.

## Risks and Watchouts
- creating governance bureaucracy without decision speed
- adding standards without enforcement or adoption
- over-centralizing decisions
- ignoring local regulatory or operational requirements
- measuring governance activity instead of outcomes
- weakening innovation by making approval paths too slow
