Q4 — Annotated glossary: Chinese operational execution terminology

Purpose: a structured glossary of Chinese terms used to describe rapid corporate execution. Each entry below separates: Term (Chinese), Pinyin, Literal translation, Corporate usage, Documented example / source, and Operational significance.

## DOMAIN 1 — Military / mobilization language
### 战役 — Zhànyì (Campaign)
- Term: 战役
- Pinyin: Zhànyì
- Literal translation: military campaign
- Corporate usage: a time‑bound, company‑wide initiative with a named commander, dedicated resources, and a war‑room governance rhythm (distinct from a routine "project").
- Documented example / source: Alibaba's "Three Great Campaigns" (三大战役); Edward Tse, China's Disruptors (2015), Ch.4.
- Operational significance: signals a governance override, daily cadence, and high‑stakes accountability.

### 战斗 — Zhàndòu (Battle)
- Term: 战斗
- Pinyin: Zhàndòu
- Literal translation: battle (single engagement)
- Corporate usage: a short, intensive push (1–4 weeks) toward a milestone; multiple battles make up a campaign.
- Documented example / source: Douyin's "100‑day battle" for launch; Chen Lin, ByteDance case (2020).
- Operational significance: compresses decision latency and accelerates resource approvals.

### 总指挥 — Zǒng Zhǐhuī (Commander‑in‑Chief)
- Term: 总指挥
- Pinyin: Zǒng Zhǐhuī
- Literal translation: overall commander
- Corporate usage: single named executive with authority to reallocate resources and make binding decisions for a campaign's duration.
- Documented example / source: Huawei's 5G campaign leadership; Tian Tao, The Huawei Story (2014).
- Operational significance: temporary structural override of matrix friction.

### 作战室 — Zuòzhàn Shì (War Room)
- Term: 作战室
- Pinyin: Zuòzhàn Shì
- Literal translation: operations / war room
- Corporate usage: co‑located or virtual control room with live dashboards, daily stand‑ups, and immediate escalation protocols.
- Documented example / source: JD.com's 6.18 war room; Liu Qiangdong, The JD.com Story (2016).
- Operational significance: enforces closed‑loop problem resolution and removes meeting latency.

### 攻坚战 — Gōngjiān Zhàn (Tough / Storming Battle)
- Term: 攻坚战
- Pinyin: Gōngjiān Zhàn
- Literal translation: assault on fortified positions
- Corporate usage: campaign to overcome entrenched obstacles; justifies concentrated resources and multi‑year commitment.
- Documented example / source: Huawei's European market push; Tian Tao (2014).
- Operational significance: authorizes resource concentration beyond normal ROI constraints.

### 突击队 — Tūjī Duì (Shock Team)
- Term: 突击队
- Pinyin: Tūjī Duì
- Literal translation: assault / shock team
- Corporate usage: small elite cross‑functional team operating outside normal lines, tasked with high‑uncertainty breakthroughs.
- Documented example / source: Alibaba Cloud founding shock team; Zeng Ming, Smart Business (2018).
- Operational significance: protects experimentation from core‑business politics.

## DOMAIN 2 — Internal competition and selection
### 赛马机制 — Sàimǎ Jīzhì (Horse‑racing mechanism)
- Term: 赛马机制
- Pinyin: Sàimǎ Jīzhì
- Literal translation: horse‑racing mechanism
- Corporate usage: fund multiple teams to pursue the same objective; the winner receives scale; losers are wound down.
- Documented example / source: Tencent teams competing to build WeChat; Wu Xiaobo, Tencent (2017).
- Operational significance: externalizes selection pressure; prioritizes ex‑post market results over ex‑ante mandates.

### 跑马圈地 — Pǎo Mǎ Quān Dì (Riding horses to encircle land)
- Term: 跑马圈地
- Pinyin: Pǎo Mǎ Quān Dì
- Literal translation: gallop to claim territory
- Corporate usage: rapid geographic or user acquisition to establish market position ahead of rivals.
- Documented example / source: Meituan city expansion (2013–2016); internal communications quoted in Caijing.
- Operational significance: prioritizes speed of occupation over near‑term profitability.

### 养蛊模式 — Yǎng Gǔ Móshì (Raising poisonous insects model)
- Term: 养蛊模式
- Pinyin: Yǎng Gǔ Móshì
- Literal translation: raising poisonous insects
- Corporate usage: pejorative term for brutal, under‑resourced internal competition (critics/journalists use it).
- Documented example / source: Caixin reporting (2019).
- Operational significance: cautionary signal—the dark side of internal competition.

## DOMAIN 3 — Frontline decision‑making and empowerment
### 让听得见炮声的人做决策 — Ràng Tīng Dé Jiàn Pào Shēng de Rén Zuò Juécè
- Term: 让听得见炮声的人做决策
- Pinyin: Ràng Tīng Dé Jiàn Pào Shēng de Rén Zuò Juécè
- Literal translation: let those who hear the cannon fire make decisions
- Corporate usage: decentralize authority to frontline managers who have direct situational awareness.
- Documented example / source: Ren Zhengfei speech, Huawei (2009); follow‑up reforms (2013).
- Operational significance: moves budget and hiring authority downward (real subsidiarity, not rhetoric).

### 班长战争 — Bānzhǎng Zhànzhēng (Squad‑leader warfare)
- Term: 班长战争
- Pinyin: Bānzhǎng Zhànzhēng
- Literal translation: squad‑level warfare
- Corporate usage: organizational model emphasizing many small autonomous teams with decision rights.
- Documented example / source: Ren Zhengfei internal document (2014); discussed in Tian Tao & De Cremer.
- Operational significance: enables distributed, rapid local decision‑making supported by platform capabilities.

### 小微 — Xiǎo Wēi (Micro‑enterprise)
- Term: 小微
- Pinyin: Xiǎo Wēi
- Literal translation: micro enterprise
- Corporate usage: Haier's Rendanheyi unit: small P&L‑responsible units (5–15 people) with decision, hire and pay rights.
- Documented example / source: Zhang Ruimin, HBR (2016).
- Operational significance: internal entrepreneurship scaled as small legal/financial units.

## DOMAIN 4 — Absorption and enforcement
### 先僵化，后固化，再优化 — Xiān Jiānghuà, Hòu Gùhuà, Zài Yōuhuà
- Term: 先僵化，后固化，再优化
- Pinyin: Xiān Jiānghuà, Hòu Gùhuà, Zài Yōuhuà
- Literal translation: first rigidify, then solidify, then optimize
- Corporate usage: impose a standard quickly (rigidify), lock it in (solidify), then iterate improvements (optimize).
- Documented example / source: documented practices at Huawei and other transform efforts (see cited literature).
- Operational significance: inverts participatory change norms—apply system first, adapt later.

### 穿美国鞋 — Chuān Měiguó Xié (Wear American shoes)
- Term: 穿美国鞋
- Pinyin: Chuān Měiguó Xié
- Literal translation: wear American shoes
- Corporate usage: adopt imported systems/processes as authoritative and conform the organization to them.
- Documented example / source: Ren Zhengfei metaphor for adopting IBM IPD; Tian Tao (2014).
- Operational significance: signals a top‑down adoption approach rather than local co‑creation.

## DOMAIN 5 — Performance accountability
### 闭环管理 — Bìhuán Guǎnlǐ (Closed‑loop management)
- Term: 闭环管理
- Pinyin: Bìhuán Guǎnlǐ
- Literal translation: closed‑loop management
- Corporate usage: same‑day or rapid loop closure expectation; visible escalation and ownership of open issues.
- Documented example / source: various company practices (see main text references).
- Operational significance: enforces moral and operational accountability for unresolved tasks.

### 一把手负责制 — Yī Bǎ Shǒu Fùzé Zhì (First‑in‑Command responsibility)
- Term: 一把手负责制
- Pinyin: Yī Bǎ Shǒu Fùzé Zhì
- Literal translation: first‑in‑command responsibility system
- Corporate usage: ultimate personal accountability sits with the top leader; that person bears cost of failure.
- Documented example / source: governance patterns across Chinese firms; cited literature.
- Operational significance: accelerates obstacle removal via concentrated personal liability.

### 结果导向 — Jiéguǒ Dǎoxiàng (Results orientation)
- Term: 结果导向
- Pinyin: Jiéguǒ Dǎoxiàng
- Literal translation: results orientation
- Corporate usage: measurable outcomes are the primary currency; effort without measurable output is discounted.
- Documented example / source: Huawei maxims and performance systems; De Cremer et al. (2017).
- Operational significance: enforces strict performance ranking and high stakes for underperformance.

### 对赌协议 — Duìdǔ Xiéyì (Bet‑on agreement / VAM)
- Term: 对赌协议
- Pinyin: Duìdǔ Xiéyì
- Literal translation: bet‑on agreement
- Corporate usage: contractual performance agreements with binary upside/downside outcomes for leaders.
- Documented example / source: Alibaba business unit restructurings (2013); Caijing reporting.
- Operational significance: creates binary incentives that accelerate decisions but can encourage short‑term risk.

## DOMAIN 6 — Rapid iteration & internal entrepreneurship
### 小步快跑 — Xiǎo Bù Kuài Pǎo (Small steps, fast running)
- Term: 小步快跑
- Pinyin: Xiǎo Bù Kuài Pǎo
- Literal translation: small steps, fast running
- Corporate usage: iterate in small, fast increments (tempo often measured in days or weeks).
- Documented example / source: WeChat early cadence; Wu Xiaobo, Tencent (2017).
- Operational significance: enables rapid feature testing and fast learning cycles.

### 野蛮生长 — Yěmán Shēngzhǎng (Barbaric growth)
- Term: 野蛮生长
- Pinyin: Yěmán Shēngzhǎng
- Literal translation: barbaric / savage growth
- Corporate usage: a growth phase where speed dominates process and compliance concerns.
- Documented example / source: Feng Lun's usage and sector descriptions; Feng Lun (2007).
- Operational significance: useful as a periodization concept—then followed by systematization.

### 内部创业 — Nèibù Chuàngyè (Internal entrepreneurship)
- Term: 内部创业
- Pinyin: Nèibù Chuàngyè
- Literal translation: internal entrepreneurship
- Corporate usage: parent company seeds ventures internally with close equity/ties and personal stakes for founders.
- Documented example / source: Huawei internal entrepreneurship programs; Tian Tao (2014).
- Operational significance: higher personal stakes and closer parent ties compared with Western intrapreneurship.
