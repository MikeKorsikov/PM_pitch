Q6 — Chinese books frequently read by Chinese executives on execution, management, competition and transformation

This file maps the Chinese-language literature that shapes executive thinking in China. Chinese leaders draw on domestic books as shared reference points, not just translated Western classics. When a leader cites these works, the audience reads the concept with a common set of assumptions.

Each entry is organized consistently with:
- title (Chinese / pinyin / English)
- author(s)
- publication
- type
- what it is
- why it matters
- documented influence / references
- publication details

## Category 1: Books that shaped Huawei's management doctrine
Huawei is the most textually self-documenting major Chinese company. Its internal publications, speech collections and analyst books form a canon that many other firms study.

### 《华为基本法》 (Huáwéi Jīběn Fǎ) — The Huawei Basic Law
- Author: Huawei Technologies, directed by Ren Zhengfei; drafting led by Renmin University professor Huang Weiwei (黄卫伟)
- Publication: internal document 1998; later published by CITIC Press
- Type: corporate constitution / management charter

What it is
- A 25,000-character charter of Huawei's values, operating principles, organizational design and governance rules.
- Covers R&D policy, HR principles, organizational structure and leadership succession.
- Modeled in ambition on a constitution rather than a typical corporate handbook.

Why it matters
- Made explicit the idea that a Chinese private company should have a written management philosophy.
- Framed Huawei's corporate culture as a system rather than a founder's personality.

Documented influence
- Huawei: governed Huawei's system from 1998 until its first major revision in 2018.
- Wider influence: inspired other Chinese firms to draft their own “Basic Laws.”
- Academic recognition: formed the basis for the Huawei Management Philosophy series.

Publication details
- CITIC Press, 2001 commercial edition; drafting documented in Tian Tao, The Huawei Story (2014), Ch. 5.

### 《以奋斗者为本》 (Yǐ Fèndòu Zhě Wéi Běn) — By the Strivers, For the Strivers
- Editor: Huang Weiwei (黄卫伟) and the Renmin University Huawei research team
- Publication: CITIC Press, 2014
- Type: speech collection / HR philosophy volume

What it is
- Official Huawei volume compiling Ren Zhengfei's speeches and HR documents on talent management.
- Defines the “striver” (奋斗者) as a formal Huawei category with higher expectations and rewards.

Why it matters
- Makes Huawei's meritocratic yet demanding culture explicit.
- Establishes the logic that sacrifice and performance are the core currency.

Documented influence
- Huawei: operationalized through the “striver agreement” (奋斗者协议).
- Wider influence: the phrase has entered China’s business lexicon as shorthand for high-commitment culture.
- Referenced by: Richard Liu (JD.com) praised Huawei’s striver culture in a 2015 speech; Jack Ma has used similar language.

Publication details
- ISBN: 978-7-5086-4722-4; CITIC Press, 2014; 320 pages.

### 《以客户为中心》 (Yǐ Kèhù Wéi Zhōngxīn) — Customer-Centricity
- Editor: Huang Weiwei (黄卫伟) and the Renmin University team
- Publication: CITIC Press, 2016
- Type: strategic doctrine volume

What it is
- Companion volume to By the Strivers.
- Compiles Huawei strategy materials emphasizing customer value over technology push.

Why it matters
- Establishes Huawei’s operating principle: customer-first, technology-second.
- Reinforces the outward strategic pillar that balances Huawei’s inward striver culture.

Documented influence
- Huawei: central to its strategy and product development mindset.
- Referenced by: Lei Jun (Xiaomi) cited the volume when describing Xiaomi's move toward more disciplined supply-chain and quality management.

Publication details
- ISBN: 978-7-5086-6356-0; CITIC Press, 2016; 340 pages.

## Category 2: Founder autobiographies and business narratives
These books are commercial leadership epics that Chinese executives read to understand peer journeys, strategic choices and execution mindsets.

### 《腾讯传》 (Téngxùn Zhuàn) — The Tencent Story
- Author: Wu Xiaobo (吴晓波)
- Publication: CITIC Press, 2017
- Type: authorized corporate biography

What it is
- Comprehensive history of Tencent from 1998 to its internet platform leadership.
- Based on interviews with Pony Ma, Allen Zhang and key Tencent executives.

Why it matters
- Documents Tencent’s execution patterns, especially internal competition and platform strategy.
- Normalizes “horse-racing” selection and the shift from closed empire to open platform.

Documented influence
- Widely read across Chinese tech firms.
- Referenced by ByteDance, Meituan and other internet executives.

Publication details
- ISBN: 978-7-5086-7148-1; CITIC Press, 2017; 480 pages.
- English edition: Tencent: The Story of a Chinese Internet Giant, translated by Martha Avery, 2018.

### 《创京东》 (Chuàng Jīngdōng) — Creating JD.com
- Author: Li Zhigang (李志刚)
- Publication: CITIC Press, 2015
- Type: authorized corporate biography

What it is
- The story of JD.com's rise from a Beijing electronics stall to a logistics and e-commerce leader.
- Based on 250+ hours of interviews with Liu Qiangdong and JD employees.

Why it matters
- Details bold execution choices like building JD’s logistics network and operating a 6.18 war room.
- Highlights a founder-led, detail-driven culture.

Documented influence
- Used in JD.com management training.
- Referenced by Colin Huang (Pinduoduo) as inspiration and contrast for asset-light strategy.

Publication details
- ISBN: 978-7-5086-5151-4; CITIC Press, 2015; 320 pages.
- English edition: The JD.com Story, translated by Martha Avery, 2016.

### 《野蛮生长》 (Yěmán Shēngzhǎng) — Barbaric Growth
- Author: Feng Lun (冯仑)
- Publication: CITIC Press, 2007
- Type: founder memoir / business philosophy

What it is
- Feng Lun’s autobiography of founding Vantone Real Estate in the early 1990s.
- Introduces “barbaric growth” as a phase of chaotic, aggressive expansion.

Why it matters
- Popularized a widely used Chinese business concept describing early-stage growth.
- Frames speed and market capture as phase-based strategy rather than reckless behavior.

Documented influence
- Cited by entrepreneurs from Feng Lun’s generation.
- Praised by Wang Shi (Vanke) and often paired with books by reform-era founders.

Publication details
- ISBN: 978-7-5086-0884-3; CITIC Press, 2007; 280 pages.

### 《道路与梦想》 (Dàolù Yǔ Mèngxiǎng) — The Road and the Dream
- Author: Wang Shi (王石)
- Co-author: Miao Shi (缪诗)
- Publication: CITIC Press, 2014
- Type: founder autobiography

What it is
- Wang Shi’s story of building Vanke and professionalizing China’s real estate business.
- Connects leadership, governance and ethics through personal narrative.

Why it matters
- Documents the first-in-command responsibility system and professionalization campaign.
- Provides an early Chinese example of founder-led institutionalization.

Documented influence
- Used in Vanke management training.
- Cited by Zhang Ruimin (Haier) and other manufacturing executives.

Publication details
- ISBN: 978-7-5086-4385-7; CITIC Press, 2014; 380 pages.

## Category 3: Strategy and competition theory
These works articulate strategic frameworks and competitive logic used by Chinese executives.

### 《智能商业》 (Zhìnéng Shāngyè) — Smart Business
- Author: Zeng Ming (曾鸣)
- Publication: Harvard Business Review Press (English), 2018; CITIC Press (Chinese), 2018
- Type: practitioner strategy framework

What it is
- Zeng Ming’s synthesis of Alibaba-era strategy, focusing on platform coordination and data intelligence.
- Presents the Middle Platform (中台) and smart business model.

Why it matters
- Defines strategic logic behind many Chinese digital ecosystems.
- Explains why data and platform architecture are core competitive assets.

Documented influence
- Alibaba’s strategic doctrine under Daniel Zhang.
- Widely adopted across Chinese tech and enterprise software companies.

Publication details
- English edition: Smart Business: What Alibaba's Success Reveals About the Future of Strategy, HBR Press, 2018. ISBN: 978-1-63369-329-6; 320 pages.
- Chinese edition: 《智能商业》, CITIC Press, 2018.

### 《颠覆者》 (Diānfù Zhě) — China’s Disruptors
- Author: Edward Tse (谢祖墀)
- Publication: Portfolio (English), 2015; CITIC Press (Chinese), 2015
- Type: analytical overview

What it is
- Analysis of how Chinese firms compete through speed, cost innovation and boundary-blurring.
- Covers Alibaba, Tencent, Baidu, Huawei, Xiaomi, Haier and others.

Why it matters
- Frames Chinese disruption as a distinct model from Western Christensen-style disruption.
- Emphasizes the need for constant reinvention in China’s fast markets.

Documented influence
- Read by foreign and Chinese executives as a mirror on Chinese strategy.
- Used in MBA programs at CEIBS and CKGSB.

Publication details
- English: China's Disruptors, Portfolio, 2015. ISBN: 978-1-59184-754-0.
- Chinese: 《颠覆者》, CITIC Press, 2015.

## Category 4: Organizational transformation
These books describe transformation models and the operational mechanics of major Chinese organizational shifts.

### 《海尔新模式》 (Hǎi'ěr Xīn Móshì) — Reinventing Giants
- Authors: Bill Fischer, Umberto Lago and Fang Liu (IMD)
- Publication: Jossey-Bass (English), 2013; CITIC Press (Chinese), 2013
- Type: academic case study

What it is
- Detailed study of Haier’s transformation to the Rendanheyi model.
- Examines governance, compensation, and platform organization.

Why it matters
- Provides one of the clearest external accounts of Haier’s transformation.
- Makes the ZZJYT / Xiaowei mechanics concrete.

Documented influence
- Used in Haier’s external communications.
- Widely cited in Western business schools.

Publication details
- English: Reinventing Giants: How Chinese Global Competitor Haier Has Changed the Way Big Companies Transform, Jossey-Bass, 2013. ISBN: 978-1-118-60223-8.
- Chinese: 《海尔新模式》, CITIC Press, 2013.

### 《张瑞敏：自以为非》 (Zhāng Ruìmǐn: Zì Yǐwéi Fēi) — Zhang Ruimin: Self-Negation
- Author: Zhang Ruimin (张瑞敏); edited by Haier’s internal culture department
- Publication: CITIC Press, 2019
- Type: thematic speech and idea collection

What it is
- A curated collection of Zhang Ruimin’s speeches and interviews.
- Centers on the philosophy of self-negation and continuous disruption.

Why it matters
- Captures the intellectual core of Haier’s leadership style.
- Shows how Chinese transformation rhetoric is sustained by narrative and metaphor.

Documented influence
- Serves as an internal reference for Haier employees.
- Cited widely in Chinese manufacturing and operations media.

Publication details
- ISBN: 978-7-5086-9962-1; CITIC Press, 2019; 420 pages.
