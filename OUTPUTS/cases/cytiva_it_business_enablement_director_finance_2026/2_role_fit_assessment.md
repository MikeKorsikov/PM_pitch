Senior Finance IT/business enablement role responsible for aligning Finance priorities with IT capabilities, defining Finance IT strategy and application roadmaps, supporting value stream prioritization, driving POCs and requirements, and ensuring measurable business outcomes across Finance technology transformation.

- Business/IT translation and senior stakeholder influence.

- Value stream prioritization and backlog alignment.

## Seniority Fit
Aligned. The role is director-level, Finance-function facing, and transformation/value oriented. It matches the candidate's senior finance systems product and transformation profile better than narrow hands-on platform-administration roles.

## Underqualification Signals
- Direct Cytiva/Danaher Business System experience is not evidenced.
- Direct life-sciences GxP/CSV validation leadership is not evidenced.
- Direct M&A/due diligence experience needs validation.
- Specific Cytiva ERP/Finance/Data platform knowledge is unknown.

## Overqualification Signals
- Low risk. Role appears senior enough to use the candidate's transformation, governance, stakeholder, and roadmap experience.

## Acceptable Gaps
- GxP/regulatory specifics, if the role focuses on governance awareness rather than validation leadership.
- Danaher Business System, if continuous improvement experience is sufficient.
- M&A due diligence, because it is a plus.
- Specific Cytiva platform knowledge, if the role is business enablement and roadmap-focused.

## Red Flag Gaps
- If the role requires direct life-sciences validation ownership from day one.
- If the role is mainly an internal Danaher operating model specialist rather than Finance IT enablement.
- If the role requires hands-on configuration ownership of named finance platforms not in the JD.
