# Capability Impact Map - Cytiva IT Business Enablement Director - Finance

## Purpose
Map likely pain points to business capabilities, technology capabilities, and transformation opportunities.

| Pain Point | Business Capabilities | Technology Capabilities | Transformation Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| fragmented_governance | finance governance, portfolio prioritization, stakeholder management | roadmap management, delivery governance, backlog management | Create clearer interface between Finance and IT with value-based prioritization. |
| integration_complexity | finance operations, reporting, planning, controls | integration management, ERP landscape management, data platform governance | Improve visibility across ERPs, finance systems, and analytics platforms. |
| poor_e2e_visibility | decision support, performance management, Finance leadership reporting | analytics, KPI reporting, data lineage | Improve outcome tracking, ROI visibility, and decision support. |
| low_automation | process improvement, operational excellence | workflow automation, analytics enablement, AI-assisted productivity | Identify practical automation opportunities across Finance processes. |
| compliance_audit_complexity | risk management, compliance management, quality/regulatory governance | auditability, access control, evidence management | Ensure Finance IT change remains compliant and risk-aware. |

## Architecture Implications
- Finance landscape likely includes multiple platforms and value streams.
- Integration, data ownership, prioritization, and delivery governance are central.
- Role requires business architecture and product governance thinking more than narrow tool administration.

## Transformation Opportunities
- Finance technology roadmap rationalization.
- Value stream backlog governance.
- Process and application landscape visibility.
- Automation and analytics use-case prioritization.
- Compliance-aware delivery model.
