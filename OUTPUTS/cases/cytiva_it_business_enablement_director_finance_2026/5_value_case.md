# Value Case - Cytiva IT Business Enablement Director - Finance

## Purpose
Connect role-relevant transformation opportunities to business value.

## Selected Value Models
- governance_maturity
- operational_efficiency
- ai_adoption_value
- automation_roi

## Business Outcomes
- Better alignment between Finance priorities and IT delivery.
- Clearer Finance application roadmap and investment prioritization.
- Faster decision-making through improved data and analytics.
- Reduced manual effort through automation and process optimization.
- Better compliance readiness through governance, risk, and evidence discipline.
- Improved delivery predictability through value stream and product-centric execution.

## KPIs and Evidence Signals
- roadmap delivery predictability
- value stream backlog throughput
- benefits realization / ROI tracking
- Finance process cycle time
- automation coverage
- stakeholder satisfaction
- audit/control findings
- incident or escalation trends
- data/reporting quality issues

## Value Assumptions
- Actual Cytiva baseline metrics are unknown.
- Do not calculate financial value without company data.
- Use value model as interview framing, not as claimed savings.
