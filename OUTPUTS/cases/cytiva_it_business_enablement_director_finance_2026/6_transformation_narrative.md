# Transformation Narrative - Cytiva IT Business Enablement Director - Finance

## Purpose
Create concise, role-aligned narrative for recruiter and future interviews.

## Executive Version
The role appears to sit at the intersection of Finance leadership, IT delivery, product governance, data/analytics, automation, and regulated operational excellence. The core challenge is not simply running applications; it is ensuring Finance technology investments are aligned to business outcomes, prioritized through value streams, and delivered in a way that improves efficiency, quality, cost, decision-making, and compliance.

## Role-Fit Version
My strongest fit is in finance systems product ownership and transformation. I have owned roadmaps, backlogs, governance forums, support models, vendor delivery, risk/change processes, and stakeholder communication across finance technology platforms. I am strongest where Finance, IT, reporting, controls, data flows, and operating model need to be aligned into practical delivery priorities.

## Application-Safe Version
I bring senior experience connecting Finance process needs with technology roadmaps, delivery governance, and measurable business outcomes. My background spans finance systems product ownership, transformation, process improvement, stakeholder management, vendor governance, reporting/data-flow awareness, and controls-focused delivery. For Cytiva, the attractive part of the role is the chance to help Finance turn priorities into scalable, compliant, value-driven IT outcomes.

## AI and Automation Lens
AI and automation should be positioned as practical Finance productivity and decision-support enablers, not as hype:
- identify repetitive/manual finance workflows
- improve reporting and insight generation
- support knowledge management and process standardization
- improve decision support and prioritization
- keep human validation, compliance, and data governance in place
