I have owned roadmaps, backlogs, governance forums, support models, vendor delivery, risk and change processes, and stakeholder communication across finance technology platforms. What interests me in this role is the business enablement angle: connecting Finance priorities with IT capabilities, value streams, analytics, automation, and measurable outcomes.

# How do you translate Finance priorities into technology delivery? | 
"I start by clarifying the business outcome, affected Finance process, value, risk, data requirement, control implication, and stakeholder ownership. Then I translate that into roadmap items, backlog priorities, requirements, delivery approach, acceptance criteria, and measurable outcomes." |

# How do you prioritize competing Finance demands? | 
"I look at business value, risk, compliance, operational impact, cost, dependency, urgency, capacity, and strategic alignment. I try to make trade-offs visible so Finance and IT can make informed decisions rather than allowing the loudest stakeholder to set priority." |

| What is your experience with agile or product-centric delivery? | 
"I have implemented and worked with agile/SDLC ways of working, Jira backlogs, epics, stories, prioritization, ceremonies, testing, release governance, and change controls. My style is pragmatic: use product/agile methods to improve clarity and delivery, not to create process theatre." |

# How do you track ROI or value after delivery? | 
"I define the expected outcome early: time saved, risk reduced, quality improved, faster reporting, better decision-making, lower operating cost, or improved compliance. After delivery, I would compare actual adoption and results against the promise, using practical measures rather than artificial precision." |

# How do you approach automation in Finance? | 
"I start with process understanding. Automation is useful when the process is repeatable, rules-based, measurable, and controlled. If the process is unclear, fragmented, or poorly owned, automation can make the problem faster rather than better." |

# What is your GxP or regulated-environment experience? | 
"I have strong controls, audit, access governance, documentation, and compliance-oriented delivery experience. I would be careful not to overstate direct GxP validation leadership. I understand the discipline required in regulated environments and would want to understand how deep the GxP expectation is for this role." |

| Do you have M&A or due diligence experience? | 
"I would need to clarify the level expected. I have worked in complex transformation, platform transition, operating model, and finance systems governance contexts, but I would not overclaim direct M&A due diligence ownership unless a specific example is validated." |

| What would you want to understand first in the role? | 
"The Finance technology landscape, current roadmaps, value streams, stakeholder model, decision rights, major pain points, regulatory constraints, and how success is measured in the first 6 to 12 months." |

## Risks to Manage
- Do not overclaim Danaher Business System experience.
- Do not overclaim GxP validation or CSV leadership.
- Do not claim Cytiva-specific finance platform knowledge.
- Do not present AI/automation as the main identity unless they ask.
- Keep the center of gravity: Finance + IT enablement + roadmap + value + stakeholder leadership.

## Recommended Call Strategy
- Lead with finance systems product ownership and transformation.
- Keep examples senior, not tool-heavy.
- Clarify role scope early.
- Ask about platforms and decision rights.
- Position GxP as controls/regulatory discipline unless they need specialist depth.
- Treat this as a strong-fit opportunity.
