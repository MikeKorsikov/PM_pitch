## Role Summary
The role bridges Finance business objectives with IT capabilities to deliver measurable business outcomes for the Finance enabling function.

The role is positioned as a senior IT/business enablement role focused on:
- Finance IT strategy and roadmaps
- business and IT alignment
- Finance application landscape improvement
- product-centric and agile delivery models
- value stream prioritization
- analytics, automation, and digital innovation
- operational excellence, risk, regulatory, and GxP-aware delivery

## Reporting Line
- Reports to: Sr Director IT Business Enablement Functions leader
- Team context: IT Business Enablement - Functions
- Role also acts as virtual team lead and delegate for the Sr Director ITBE Functions leader.

## Key Responsibilities
- Partner with Cytiva Finance leaders, Finance teams, Finance Systems, and IT delivery teams.
- Provide a single effective interface between business functions and IT.
- Align technology priorities with Cytiva business objectives.
- Define, execute, and maintain Finance OpCo, functional, and regional IT strategy.
- Own or shape Finance application roadmaps in line with Cytiva priorities.
- Support implementation of the Discovery model.
- Partner with Finance leaders, Finance Systems, IT Business Analysts, and BE Pillar Head.
- Identify business outcomes, assign them to value streams, and prioritize within value stream backlog.
- Partner with IT Delivery & Governance to determine delivery approach.
- Use relevant value stream execution teams to deliver business value.
- Drive proof of concepts.
- Author user requirement specifications.
- Track ROI and say/do performance after delivery.
- Ensure IT initiatives improve efficiency, cost, quality, customer satisfaction, and decision-making.
- Champion continuous improvement and innovation across Finance.
- Leverage advanced analytics, automation, digital technologies, and external market trends.
- Optimize end-to-end Finance processes.
- Maintain and improve the Finance technology landscape, including multiple ERPs, Finance ecosystems, and Data & Analytics platforms.
- Ensure robust integration, GxP/regulatory compliance, proactive risk mitigation, and operational excellence.
- Act as virtual pillar team lead, delegate to the Pillar Head, and escalation point for prioritization conflicts and stakeholder escalations.

## Required Experience
- 12+ years of experience partnering with Finance teams, supporting finance business processes, or finance technology transformation.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, STEM equivalent certification, or equivalent experience.
- Strong understanding of Finance capabilities.
- Ability to translate business priorities and data requirements into scalable technology solutions.
- Ability to influence senior stakeholders through credibility, business acumen, structured communication, and clear decision support.
- Experience operating in global, matrixed organizations with competing priorities and cross-functional delivery teams.
- Outcome-oriented mindset with resilience and accountability.
- Ability to improve consistency, efficiency, and business value through technology.

## Plus
- Experience with M&A and due diligence projects at global and cross-functional scale.
