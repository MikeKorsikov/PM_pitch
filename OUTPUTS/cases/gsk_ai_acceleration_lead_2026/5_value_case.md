# Value Case - GSK AI Acceleration Lead

## Purpose
Connect AI acceleration opportunities to business value using relevant value models.

## Selected Value Models
| Value Model | Why It Applies | Business Outcomes | Possible KPIs | User Validation Needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ai_adoption_value | Role is explicitly about AI pilots, AI-native capabilities, adoption, and scale. | Faster AI adoption, better decision support, improved operational intelligence. | AI adoption rate, active users, time saved, decision cycle time, user satisfaction. | Validate real AI use examples and safe claims. |
| operational_efficiency | Role targets workflow bottlenecks, trial delivery acceleration, time saved, and quality improvement. | Reduced manual effort, faster cycle times, improved throughput, better user experience. | cycle time, manual hours, backlog, rework, quality signals. | Validate process improvement and operational efficiency examples. |
| governance_maturity | Role requires robust, compliant, scalable pilots in regulated pharma context. | Safer AI scaling, clearer decision rights, improved compliance readiness. | approval cycle time, risk assessments, audit findings, policy compliance, scale-readiness. | Validate governance/GxP/controlled-documentation evidence. |
| automation_roi | AI pilots may automate or augment repetitive workflow tasks. | Better scalability, lower effort, reduced error, improved workforce capacity. | automation coverage, hours saved, exception rate, SLA improvement, payback logic. | Use only if user has credible automation examples or avoids numeric claims. |

## Business Outcomes
- Better prioritization of AI opportunities based on evidence.
- Faster movement from workflow bottleneck to pilot and scale decision.
- More consistent value measurement across AI pilots.
- Increased adoption of AI-enabled ways of working.
- Reduced risk of scaling poorly governed or weakly adopted AI solutions.

## KPIs and Evidence Signals
- pilot cycle time
- time saved per workflow
- quality improvement
- user adoption rate
- readiness-to-scale assessment
- risk reduction
- number of pilots stopped, iterated, or scaled with evidence
- stakeholder satisfaction
- reusable pattern adoption

## Value Assumptions
- No precise financial values should be claimed without company data.
- The application should use value categories rather than invented numbers.
- Candidate examples should focus on value logic, governance, and measurable outcomes.
- AI value should be framed as workflow augmentation and operational intelligence, not hype.
