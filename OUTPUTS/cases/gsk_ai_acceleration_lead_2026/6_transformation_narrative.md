# Transformation Narrative - GSK AI Acceleration Lead

## Purpose
Create a concise, role-aligned transformation story.

## Executive Version
AI acceleration in pharma is not only a technology challenge. It is a transformation challenge: identifying real workflow bottlenecks, designing practical pilots, proving value, governing risk, and helping users adopt new ways of working. The GSK AI Acceleration Lead role appears to sit exactly at that intersection of clinical operations, enterprise technology, vendors, portfolio decisions, and measurable business value.

## Role-Fit Version
My strongest fit for this role is the ability to translate complex business and technology environments into structured action: process discovery, capability mapping, roadmap thinking, stakeholder alignment, governance, and value realization. I would bring a practical AI-enabled transformation lens: start from workflow pain, frame testable AI pilot hypotheses, define success measures, coordinate delivery partners, and help determine what should stop, iterate, or scale.

## Application-Safe Version
Based on the job description, GSK is looking for someone who can help move AI opportunities from ideas into practical pilots and scalable adoption across Development Operations. My contribution would be strongest in connecting process analysis, product governance, stakeholder management, AI tooling awareness, and value measurement so AI pilots are not just technically interesting, but usable, governed, adopted, and linked to measurable outcomes.

## Why AI Acceleration Matters in Pharma
- Clinical development is complex, regulated, data-heavy, and dependent on many cross-functional handoffs.
- AI can create value when it reduces manual effort, improves decision support, accelerates knowledge access, or improves operational visibility.
- In pharma, AI must be responsible, explainable enough for context, governed, privacy-aware, and aligned with compliance expectations.

## Candidate Contribution
- Translate workflow bottlenecks into AI pilot hypotheses and success criteria.
- Apply product and roadmap thinking to prioritize AI opportunities.
- Use business case and portfolio logic to evaluate value and scale-readiness.
- Bridge clinical operations users, technology teams, vendors, and leadership.
- Keep adoption, governance, controls, and documentation visible from the start.
- Bring practical AI-native prototyping experience from building a CRM application for client, appointment, and finance management using AI-assisted development tools.
- Bring major product/transformation delivery experience from implementing a OnePlan solution with Board SaaS at its core and total cost over GBP 10m.

## AI and Automation Lens
- AI should augment users, improve workflow speed and quality, and support better decisions.
- Automation should focus on stable, high-friction workflows with measurable value.
- Human validation and responsible AI controls are essential in regulated contexts.

## Positioning Guardrail
The candidate should be positioned as a product/transformation leader with AI enablement and prototyping awareness, not as a clinical operations expert, GxP validation lead, or deep ML engineer.
