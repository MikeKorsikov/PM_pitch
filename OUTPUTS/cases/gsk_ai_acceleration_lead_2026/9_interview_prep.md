## Role-Fit Story
I am strongest where business process complexity, product governance, technology delivery, stakeholder alignment, and value realization meet. For this role, I would frame my value around helping AI pilots move from real workflow problems to clear hypotheses, measurable evidence, governed delivery, and practical adoption.

## Talking Points
- AI acceleration should start from operational pain, not from tools.
- Pilot success should be measured through time saved, quality improvement, user adoption, risk reduction, and readiness to scale.
- Product and portfolio thinking help prioritize which AI opportunities deserve investment.
- Governance should make AI safer and more scalable without slowing down useful experimentation.
- Process architecture helps connect users, workflows, data, systems, controls, and adoption.

## Questions for Interviewer
- Which Development Operations workflows are currently the highest priority for AI-enabled improvement?
- What evidence matters most when deciding whether a pilot should stop, iterate, or scale?
- How do clinical operations users, enterprise AI teams, vendors, and portfolio leaders collaborate today?
- What governance or GxP expectations are most important for AI pilots in this context?
- What would success look like in the first 6 months?
