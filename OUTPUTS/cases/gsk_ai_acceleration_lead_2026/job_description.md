## Role Context
The role sits within DP&I (Development Operations Platforms & Innovation), part of Development Global Clinical Operations at GSK. DP&I is building capability to deploy AI at scale across clinical operations and the wider Development Operations function.

## Job Purpose
Support the AI-native Development Operations strategy through the design and delivery of AI pilots that address operational problems in clinical development. Work with clinical operations users, vendors, and enterprise partners to shape hypotheses, test solutions, build evidence of value, and recommend what should stop, iterate, or scale.

## Key Responsibilities
- Identify AI opportunities across Development Operations by scanning workflows and engaging clinical operations teams.
- Translate bottlenecks into AI pilot hypotheses and success criteria.
- Maintain an outside-in view of emerging AI capabilities, workflow innovations, and evolving ways of working.
- Scope and run AI pilots end-to-end, including design, delivery approach, execution oversight, and scale recommendations.
- Partner with vendors and enterprise technology teams to ensure pilots are robust, compliant, usable, and positioned to scale.
- Manage delivery risks, dependencies, and partner performance across active pilots.
- Build proof-of-value cases using time saved, quality improvement, user adoption, risk reduction, and readiness to scale.
- Use AI-native development tools, including Claude Code and Codex, for hands-on use-case testing alongside technology teams and business SMEs.
- Inform portfolio and investment decisions by synthesizing pilot findings, adoption data, and operational intelligence.
- Drive adoption for priority AI-native capabilities, including workflow redesign, user readiness, and change interventions.

## Stakeholders
- R&D leaders and business owners
- Clinical operations users
- Enterprise AI and technology teams
- Vendors and delivery partners
- Portfolio and functional leaders

## Basic Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Life Sciences, Engineering, Computer Science, Business, or related field.
- 4+ years in pharma strategy, applied AI/technology, transformation, or programme delivery roles.
- Experience in clinical operations and wider clinical development.
- Experience solving ambiguous problems and turning analysis into action.
- Experience moving from problem definition through pilot design to clear recommendation with end-to-end delivery ownership.
- Experience working across users, partners, and cross-functional teams to deliver measurable outcomes.
- Experience with AI-native development tools, including frontier agentic development tools such as Claude Code and Codex, and rapid prototyping practice.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Advanced degree in a relevant discipline.
- Experience in top-tier strategy, venture build, applied AI, or builder-led digital consulting environments.
- Knowledge of industry trends and technologies shaping clinical trials.
- Experience building business cases, ROI models, or evidence for scaling new capabilities in enterprise settings.
- Understanding of GxP expectations relevant to deploying AI in clinical operations.
- Experience driving AI-enabled capabilities beyond pilot through value delivery and portfolio-level scaling.
