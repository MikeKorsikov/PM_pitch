## Role Summary
The captured job description is for GSK's AI Acceleration Lead role, not a role titled Enterprise Architect. The role sits in Development Operations Platforms & Innovation within Development Global Clinical Operations and focuses on identifying, piloting, validating, and scaling AI-enabled workflow improvements across clinical operations.

This is a strong thematic fit for AI-enabled transformation, process architecture, governance, value realization, and cross-functional delivery. It is a weaker literal fit for an Enterprise Architect application unless the user's positioning is reframed toward AI acceleration, clinical operations transformation, and governed scaling of AI capabilities.
