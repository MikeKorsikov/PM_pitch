# Value Case - GSK Enterprise Architect

## Purpose
Connect transformation opportunities to business value using relevant value models.

## Selected Value Models
| Value Model | Why It Applies | User Validation Needed |
|---|---|---|
| ai_adoption_value | The role is explicitly about AI pilots, adoption, and scaling AI-native capabilities in clinical operations. | Validate examples of AI use, prototyping, adoption, and measurable impact. |
| operational_efficiency | The posting emphasizes workflow scanning, bottlenecks, time saved, quality improvement, and trial delivery acceleration. | Validate process improvement examples and any measurable cycle-time or effort-reduction evidence. |
| governance_maturity | The role requires robust, compliant, scalable pilots across business, technology, vendors, and portfolio leaders. | Validate regulated-environment, governance, documentation, or GxP-related experience. |

## Business Outcomes
- Faster identification and testing of AI opportunities in clinical operations.
- Better evidence for deciding which pilots should stop, iterate, or scale.
- Improved workflow efficiency and reduced administrative burden where AI is appropriate.
- Stronger adoption of AI-native capabilities through user readiness and change interventions.
- Reduced scaling risk through governance, compliance, and architecture alignment.

## KPIs and Evidence Signals
- pilot cycle time
- time saved per workflow or user group
- user adoption rate
- quality improvement signals
- number of pilots stopped, iterated, or scaled with evidence
- readiness-to-scale assessment
- risk reduction or control improvement
- stakeholder satisfaction
- reuse of shared foundations or approved patterns

## Value Assumptions
- Actual financial impact cannot be calculated without internal baseline data.
- Application materials should reference value categories, not numeric claims, unless the user provides evidence.
- The strongest value language is practical: time saved, quality improved, adoption increased, risk reduced, and scale-readiness improved.
- AI value should be framed as workforce augmentation, better decisions, and operational intelligence, not hype.
