# Transformation Narrative - GSK Enterprise Architect

## Purpose
Create a concise, role-aligned transformation story.

## Executive Version
GSK's AI Acceleration Lead posting points to a practical transformation challenge: moving AI from promising ideas into governed, measurable, adopted capabilities that improve clinical operations. The value is not AI novelty; it is the ability to identify real workflow bottlenecks, test solutions responsibly, generate evidence of value, and decide what should scale.

## Role-Fit Version
My positioning for this role is strongest at the intersection of process architecture, AI-enabled workflow design, stakeholder coordination, and governance. I can help translate ambiguous operational problems into structured hypotheses, pilot scopes, success criteria, and value narratives while keeping adoption, compliance, and scalability visible from the start.

## Application-Safe Version
Based on the job description, this role needs someone who can work across clinical operations users, enterprise technology teams, vendors, and senior stakeholders to turn AI opportunities into practical, measurable outcomes. I would position my contribution around process discovery, capability mapping, AI-assisted delivery, value framing, and disciplined governance so pilots are not only innovative, but usable, compliant, and ready to scale where evidence supports it.

## Current-State Framing
- Public evidence shows GSK is investing in data, AI/ML, automation, and technology-enabled clinical trial and R&D improvement.
- The job posting suggests a need to move from workflow pain points to AI pilots, proof of value, and enterprise adoption.
- Any claim about internal architecture, systems, or confirmed pain points must remain out of external materials unless evidenced.

## Future-State Direction
- Repeatable intake and prioritization of AI opportunities.
- Clear pilot hypotheses, success criteria, and value evidence.
- Stronger links between workflow redesign, technology delivery, user adoption, and governance.
- Scalable AI capabilities supported by shared foundations, vendor coordination, and enterprise technology partnership.

## Enterprise Architecture Contribution
- Map AI opportunities to business capabilities, technology capabilities, and measurable outcomes.
- Ensure pilot design considers integration, controls, data, adoption, and scale-readiness early.
- Translate between clinical operations needs, technology delivery, vendors, and senior stakeholders.
- Provide structure without turning lightweight pilots into heavy architecture exercises.

## AI and Automation Lens
- Use AI as a practical enabler of workflow redesign, decision support, operational insight, and knowledge reuse.
- Prioritize use cases where AI can reduce manual effort, improve quality, accelerate decisions, or improve visibility.
- Keep human validation and governance in the loop for regulated clinical operations.
