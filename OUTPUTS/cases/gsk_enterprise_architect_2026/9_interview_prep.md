## Role-Fit Story
I work best where business problems, process complexity, technology delivery, and governance meet. For this role, I would frame my value around turning operational bottlenecks into clear AI pilot hypotheses, success criteria, delivery actions, and value evidence while keeping adoption and compliance visible from the start.

## Enterprise Architecture Talking Points
- Capability mapping helps ensure AI pilots support business outcomes rather than isolated experimentation.
- Architecture thinking can improve scale-readiness by considering data, integration, controls, ownership, and reuse early.
- Governance should enable faster, safer decisions, not slow down practical pilots.

## AI / Automation Talking Points
- AI should be used where it improves workflow speed, quality, decision support, or knowledge reuse.
- AI-native tools can accelerate analysis, prototyping, documentation, and delivery coordination.
- Human-in-the-loop checkpoints are important in regulated clinical operations contexts.

## Questions for Interviewer
- Which clinical operations workflows are currently the highest priority for AI-enabled improvement?
- How does GSK decide whether an AI pilot should stop, iterate, or scale?
- What evidence matters most when proving value: time saved, quality, adoption, risk reduction, or portfolio impact?
- How do enterprise AI teams, vendors, and clinical operations users currently collaborate on pilots?
- What governance expectations are most important for AI in clinical operations?
