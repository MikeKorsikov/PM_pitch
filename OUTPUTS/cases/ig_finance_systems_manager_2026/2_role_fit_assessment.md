## Seniority Fit
Strong fit, with technical-depth caveats.

The role matches the candidate's strongest career story better than most recent cases because it sits directly in finance systems, team leadership, BAU plus transformation, vendor ownership, controls, audit readiness, and finance process improvement.

The recruiter screen should clarify whether "hands-on" means:
- working manager with enough technical depth to troubleshoot and lead specialists, or
- direct expert-level AutoRek/FIS/SQL configuration and reconciliation engineering.

## Underqualification Signals
- No direct AutoRek exposure.
- No direct FIS Integrity TMS SME-level configuration/support evidence; FIS evidence is through Quantum administration at Haleon.
- SQL proficiency should be positioned carefully because strongest evidence is practical/personal querying and portfolio tracking rather than enterprise finance-system SQL troubleshooting.
- Direct fintech, trading lifecycle, or settlement process experience is not yet evidenced.

## Overqualification Signals
- Candidate may bring broader transformation, product governance, operating model, and enterprise systems experience than the role strictly requires.

## Acceptable Gaps
- Tool-specific AutoRek knowledge may be learnable if IG values broader finance systems leadership and reconciliation platform ownership.
- FIS Quantum administration provides relevant adjacent FIS/treasury-system exposure, but not FIS Integrity TMS SME depth.
- Fintech/trading lifecycle is desirable, not listed as mandatory.
- SQL depth may be acceptable if the role has systems specialists and analysts, and the manager is expected to guide rather than write complex SQL daily.

## Red Flag Gaps
- If the role requires expert hands-on AutoRek/FIS configuration from day one.
- If the role requires advanced SQL troubleshooting as a primary daily task.
- If trading lifecycle and settlement knowledge is treated as mandatory.

## Positioning Opportunities
- Lead with finance systems ownership, not generic transformation.
- Present yourself as a working manager who can lead teams, own platforms, manage BAU, resolve issues, and still drive modernization.
- Use Cadency application ownership as the lead reconciliation story.
- Use SAP FC as the second-best consolidation/reporting story.
- Use FIS carefully: Quantum administrator at Haleon, not FIS Integrity TMS SME.
- Be honest on AutoRek/FIS specifics and position fast learning through adjacent finance systems and reconciliation/control experience.
