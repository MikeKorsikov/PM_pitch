# Capability Impact Map - IG Group Finance Systems Manager

## Purpose
Map likely pain points to business capabilities, technology capabilities, and interview-relevant talking points.

## Pain Point to Capability Map
| Pain Point | Business Capabilities | Technology Capabilities | Interview Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|
| manual_reconciliation | reconciliation management, treasury management, record to report, financial controls | reconciliation platforms, matching rules, exception workflows, audit trails | Talk about reducing manual effort and improving control reliability. |
| integration_complexity | finance operations, consolidation, reporting, treasury, management reporting | integration management, data-flow governance, system configuration, SQL/data analysis | Talk about understanding dependencies and leading specialists. |
| poor_e2e_visibility | finance leadership reporting, operations management, performance management | reporting platforms, dashboards, operational monitoring, service management | Talk about faster, more reliable insight. |
| fragmented_governance | finance systems governance, compliance, audit readiness, vendor governance | change management, access controls, documentation, GRC workflows | Talk about governance as speed plus control, not bureaucracy. |
| slow_change | transformation management, product roadmap, service management | SDLC, release governance, backlog management, vendor delivery | Talk about modernization while protecting BAU. |

## Architecture Implications
- Finance systems need clear data lineage, reconciliation ownership, integration visibility, change controls, and audit evidence.
- BAU stability and transformation must be governed together.
- Vendor and platform upgrades should be tied to roadmap value, supportability, and control impact.

## Transformation Opportunities
- Improve reconciliation exception handling.
- Standardize finance systems change governance.
- Improve data quality and insight reliability.
- Reduce manual work in close, reconciliation, and reporting processes.
- Strengthen product roadmap and vendor delivery governance.
