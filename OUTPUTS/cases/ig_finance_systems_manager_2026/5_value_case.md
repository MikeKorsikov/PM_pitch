# Value Case - IG Group Finance Systems Manager

## Purpose
Connect the role to business value for recruiter and interview discussion.

## Selected Value Models
- operational_efficiency
- governance_maturity
- automation_roi

## Business Outcomes
- More reliable finance systems operations.
- Faster and more controlled month-end / year-end periods.
- Reduced reconciliation effort and exception aging.
- Better audit readiness and control evidence.
- Stronger vendor delivery and upgrade governance.
- More scalable finance operating model through digitisation and automation.

## KPIs and Evidence Signals
- system availability
- SLA compliance
- incident volume and aging
- reconciliation exception volume
- first-pass match rate
- month-end / year-end issue volume
- audit findings
- change success rate
- release cycle time
- manual effort hours
- vendor delivery performance
