# Transformation Narrative - IG Group Finance Systems Manager

## Purpose
Create a concise, role-aligned story for recruiter-screen use.

## Executive Version
Finance systems transformation only works when operational stability, controls, data quality, vendor delivery, and roadmap priorities are managed together. The role matters because IG needs finance systems that remain reliable during critical reporting cycles while also becoming more automated, scalable, and insight-driven.

## Role-Fit Version
My background sits very close to this role: finance systems product ownership, team leadership, roadmap and backlog governance, vendor management, support model ownership, finance controls, and process improvement. I have worked across planning, reporting, consolidation, SAP, Board, Power BI, Azure, ServiceNow, Jira, Confluence, access, controls, and change governance. The strongest fit is the combination of BAU ownership and transformation: keeping systems stable while improving process, data, governance, and delivery.

## Application-Safe Version
The role makes sense because it combines finance systems leadership, operational reliability, controls, vendor management, and modernization. My strongest contribution would be bringing structure to priorities, leading the team through BAU and change, keeping audit/control expectations visible, and helping finance systems become more scalable and easier to operate.

## Technical Caveat
Tool-specific AutoRek/FIS and advanced SQL depth should be discussed honestly. Position the candidate as a strong finance systems working manager with adjacent platform, data-flow, configuration, and troubleshooting experience.
