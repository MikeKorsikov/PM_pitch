## Opening Positioning
My background is strongest in finance systems product ownership and transformation. I have led teams and platforms across planning, reporting, consolidation, finance controls, vendor delivery, support models, and change governance. What interests me in this role is the combination of BAU responsibility and modernization: keeping critical finance systems stable while improving data quality, automation, controls, and scalability.

## Core Stories to Prepare
- Reconciliation ownership story: Cadency application ownership as the lead example.
- Consolidation/reporting story: SAP FC as the second-best example.
- Finance systems ownership story: OnePlan / Board as supporting product/platform ownership evidence if needed.
- Team leadership story: managing specialists, analysts, developers, testers, SAP/data/security experts.
- BAU operations story: SLAs, incidents, service reviews, support model, month-end/year-end pressure.
- Controls story: audit readiness, access, SOX/control evidence, documentation, change approvals.
- Vendor story: challenging estimates, scope, upgrades, supportability, and value.
- Transformation story: process optimization, automation, data quality, roadmap, operating model.
- Technical honesty story: how you lead technical work without claiming expert-level depth in every platform.
