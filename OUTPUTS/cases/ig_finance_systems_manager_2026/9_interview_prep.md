# Interview Preparation - IG Group Finance Systems Manager

## Purpose
Prepare for the first recruiter call scheduled on 2026-08-19.

## Recruiter
- Name: Aaron Phillip
- Email: Aaron.Phillip@ig.com
- Duration: 30 minutes

## Recruiter Screen Objective
- Confirm role scope, expectations, and process.
- Position the candidate as a strong finance systems working manager.
- Clarify the level of hands-on AutoRek/FIS/SQL expected.
- Avoid overclaiming platform-specific expertise.
- Secure progression to hiring-manager interview.

## Opening Positioning
My background is strongest in finance systems product ownership and transformation. I have led teams and platforms across planning, reporting, consolidation, finance controls, vendor delivery, support models, and change governance. What interests me in this role is the combination of BAU responsibility and modernization: keeping critical finance systems stable while improving data quality, automation, controls, and scalability.

## Core Stories to Prepare
- Reconciliation ownership story: Cadency application ownership as the lead example.
- Consolidation/reporting story: SAP FC as the second-best example.
- Finance systems ownership story: OnePlan / Board as supporting product/platform ownership evidence if needed.
- Team leadership story: managing specialists, analysts, developers, testers, SAP/data/security experts.
- BAU operations story: SLAs, incidents, service reviews, support model, month-end/year-end pressure.
- Controls story: audit readiness, access, SOX/control evidence, documentation, change approvals.
- Vendor story: challenging estimates, scope, upgrades, supportability, and value.
- Transformation story: process optimization, automation, data quality, roadmap, operating model.
- Technical honesty story: how you lead technical work without claiming expert-level depth in every platform.

## Questions to Ask Recruiter
- How technical is the first hiring-manager interview likely to be?
- How central are AutoRek and FIS Integrity TMS to the day-to-day role?
- Is direct tool expertise required from day one, or is broader finance systems leadership acceptable?
- Would Cadency reconciliation ownership be considered relevant experience for the reconciliation side of the role?
- Is FIS Quantum administration relevant to the FIS/TMS expectations, or is the need specifically FIS Integrity TMS?
- What is the team size and structure?
- Which systems are most business-critical for the role?
- What are the main priorities for the first 6 months?
- Is the role more BAU stabilization, transformation, or both?
- What is the expected office pattern in Krakow?
- What are the next steps after the recruiter call?

## Risks to Manage
- Do not overclaim AutoRek.
- Do not overclaim FIS Integrity TMS configuration; say FIS Quantum administrator, not SME.
- Do not overclaim SQL as expert-level; use the Binance/desktop app example as practical SQL evidence.
- Do not sound too generic transformation-focused; keep finance systems at the center.
- Do not avoid the hands-on question; answer it as working-manager technical leadership.

## Recommended Call Strategy
- Lead with finance systems leadership.
- Lead reconciliation evidence with Cadency, then SAP FC.
- Use direct people-management evidence.
- Use BAU plus transformation as the main theme.
- Ask early how tool-specific the role is.
- Keep answers concise and practical.
- Treat this as a strong opportunity.
