## Company Context
IG Group is a global fintech company headquartered in London. The JD describes IG as a FTSE 100 member providing online trading platforms and access to approximately 19,000 financial markets across shares, forex, indices, and commodities.

The JD states that IG is undergoing transformation under CEO Breon Corcoran, focused on:
- delivering quality product to customers
- embedding a high-performance culture
- becoming more efficient and scalable through digitisation
- growing user base and revenue sustainably

## Role Summary
The Finance Systems Manager leads the team and platforms that keep IG's finance function running. The role combines:
- finance systems team leadership
- BAU operational excellence
- systems estate transformation
- vendor ownership
- financial controls and regulatory compliance
- finance systems modernization
- direct technical involvement

## Team Context
The role sits within Finance Systems & Change, part of global Finance.

The team owns the finance technology landscape, including:
- BAU operations across finance systems
- enhancements and new solutions
- digitisation of finance
- platform modernization
- data-quality improvement
- faster and more reliable finance insight

The role leads systems specialists and analysts, including:
- Treasury & Reconciliation support analysts
- Consolidation & Reporting support analysts
