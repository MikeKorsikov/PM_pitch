## Role Summary
This is a software engineering management role in the Dealer Channel AI Solutions area. The role leads a software delivery team expanding an existing AI and Automation platform to Europe and adapting it for European dealer business needs.

The JD emphasizes technical leadership, production software delivery, web/server application development, software engineering practices, DevOps, CI/CD, cloud platforms, team management, mentoring, and talent development.

This is not only an AI adoption or transformation role. It is a stronger fit for someone with demonstrated software development team leadership and production engineering delivery experience.

## Key Requirements Extracted
- Lead a software delivery team expanding an existing AI Platform to Europe.
- Expand the platform for European dealer-specific business needs.
- Provide engineering leadership in web application development.
- Define design best practices.
- Use the existing AI and Automation platform.
- Analyze application performance.
- Collaborate with Engineering Leads, Product Managers, Architects, UX, and supporting teams.
- Define product requirements and application designs.
- Mentor, coach, and develop direct reports.
- Provide performance and career development feedback.
- Attract, retain, and grow engineering talent.

- Demonstrate experience leading software development teams.
- Demonstrate experience as a primary technical team leader / mentor.
- Demonstrate experience building web-client or server-side applications / services.
- Demonstrate Agile, TDD, CI/CD, pipeline automation, monitoring, and DevOps experience.
- Demonstrate cloud platform experience such as AWS or Azure.

## Underqualification Signals
- Direct software development team leadership is not yet clearly evidenced.
- Primary technical mentoring of software engineers is not yet clearly evidenced.
- Production web-client or server-side application delivery is limited or not yet evidenced beyond the CRM project.
- TDD, CI/CD, build/test pipeline automation, monitoring, and DevOps experience are not yet clearly evidenced.
- Hands-on AWS/Azure delivery appears limited.
- Direct AI platform engineering ownership is not evidenced.
- Direct manufacturing / dealer channel AI solution delivery is not evidenced.

## Overqualification Signals
- Candidate may bring broader enterprise transformation, governance, process, and stakeholder experience than a narrowly hands-on engineering management role requires.

## Acceptable Gaps
- Manufacturing and dealer channel domain can be learned if the team values transferable delivery and business/technology translation.
- AI platform depth may be acceptable if the role has strong Engineering Leads and Architects supporting deep technical decisions.
- Cloud/DevOps gaps may be manageable if the role is more managerial than hands-on.

## Red Flag Gaps
- If the hiring manager requires a proven engineering manager of software developers.
- If the role requires hands-on ownership of production web/server architecture.
- If the role requires direct CI/CD, DevOps, pipeline, and monitoring delivery leadership.
- If the role requires production AI platform engineering depth.

## Positioning Opportunities
- Lead with people, delivery governance, requirements, stakeholder alignment, and business value.
- Present AI experience as practical enablement and applied tooling, not research or deep ML engineering.
- Use the CRM application as evidence of building a working application with AI tools and iterating with a real business user.
- Be direct about gaps and ask whether the role needs hands-on engineering management or broader technical delivery leadership.
- Position as someone who can help engineering teams stay connected to business outcomes and practical adoption.
