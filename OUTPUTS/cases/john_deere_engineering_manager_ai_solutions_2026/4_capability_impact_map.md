# Capability Impact Map - John Deere Engineering Manager - AI Solutions

## Purpose
Map preliminary role themes to capabilities for recruiter interview preparation.

## Pain Point to Capability Map
| Theme | Business Capabilities | Technology Capabilities | Interview Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI adoption and enablement | process management, change management, operational excellence | AI solution evaluation, workflow automation, data and analytics | Explain how AI must be connected to real work, not treated as novelty. |
| Delivery governance | portfolio management, product management, stakeholder management | delivery tooling, lifecycle management, technical documentation | Emphasize structured delivery, prioritization, and governance. |
| Integration complexity | enterprise architecture, process architecture, operating model design | APIs, integrations, data flows, cloud fundamentals | Discuss asking good questions about architecture and maintainability. |
| Knowledge reuse | knowledge management, training and onboarding, service management | knowledge assistant, document indexing, retrieval | Position as a possible AI opportunity, not confirmed company issue. |

## Architecture Implications
- AI solutions must fit existing systems, data flows, security, and operating model.
- Engineering decisions should balance value, speed, cost, maintainability, and adoption.
- Governance should enable delivery, not slow it down.

## Transformation Opportunities
- Move AI from isolated experiments to governed, reusable delivery patterns.
- Improve prioritization so AI work solves real business problems.
- Use process and capability thinking to select high-value AI use cases.
