# Value Case - John Deere Engineering Manager - AI Solutions

## Purpose
Connect likely AI solutions leadership themes to business value for interview preparation.

## Selected Value Models
- ai_adoption_value
- operational_efficiency
- governance_maturity

## Business Outcomes
- Faster movement from idea to useful AI-enabled workflow.
- Better prioritization of AI work based on value, feasibility, and adoption.
- Reduced manual effort through targeted automation and decision support.
- Improved consistency, governance, and maintainability of AI solutions.
- Better alignment between business needs and technical delivery.

## KPIs and Evidence Signals
- AI use case adoption rate.
- Time from idea to pilot.
- Time from pilot to production or operational use.
- User satisfaction / workflow adoption.
- Manual effort reduction.
- Number of reusable solution components or patterns.
- Reduction in duplicated AI experiments.

## Value Assumptions
To validate in interview:
- Whether the role focuses on delivery acceleration, team leadership, platform governance, or business adoption.
- Whether John Deere measures AI value by productivity, product capability, engineering efficiency, operations, or customer outcomes.
- Whether the recruiter can clarify success measures for the role.
