# Transformation Narrative - John Deere Engineering Manager - AI Solutions

## Purpose
Create a concise, recruiter-safe role narrative.

## Current-State Framing
John Deere is expanding Dealer Channel AI Solutions to European dealers. Based on the JD, the role is about scaling an existing AI and Automation platform while leading a software delivery team and maintaining strong engineering practices.

This means the challenge is not only AI adoption. It is also software delivery, platform extension, engineering quality, team leadership, and adapting solutions to European dealer needs.

## Future-State Direction
The future state is a reliable AI and Automation platform that can support European dealers, fit local business needs, and remain maintainable through good engineering practices, delivery discipline, and clear collaboration between product, engineering, architecture, UX, and supporting teams.

## Candidate Contribution
My safest positioning is:
- business and technology translation
- stakeholder and vendor management
- people and resource management
- delivery governance
- requirements elaboration
- process and operating model improvement
- AI adoption and practical AI tooling
- structured, pragmatic delivery

My stretch areas are:
- direct software development team leadership
- production web/server application engineering
- CI/CD, TDD, DevOps, monitoring, and cloud delivery ownership
- production AI platform engineering

## AI and Automation Lens
AI should be positioned as a practical way to improve workflows, decisions, and business operations. For this role, the stronger question is how AI solutions are engineered, adopted, supported, and scaled for dealer use.
