## Role
Engineering Manager - AI Solutions

## Location
Poznan, Poland

## Context
John Deere is expanding Dealer Channel AI Solutions to European dealers and is seeking an Engineering Manager for Dealer Channel AI Solutions.

## Role Summary
The role leads a software delivery team enabling the expansion of an existing AI and Automation platform to Europe and adapting the platform for European dealer business needs.

The Engineering Manager is described as a technical leader responsible for:
- designing and developing production software
- championing software development practices
- mentoring and coaching team members
- delivering high-quality AI and automation software solutions

## Responsibilities
- Lead a software delivery team to enable expansion of the existing AI Platform to Europe.
- Expand the platform for European-specific business needs.
- Provide engineering leadership in web application development.
- Define design best practices.
- Use the existing AI and Automation platform.
- Analyze application performance.
- Collaborate with Engineering Leads, Product Managers, Architects, User Experience, and supporting teams.
- Define product requirements and application designs.
- Mentor, coach, and develop direct reports.
- Provide ongoing feedback and guidance on performance and career development.
- Attract, retain, and grow engineering talent.
- Lead organizational change through adoption of strategic technologies, architectures, best practices, and tooling.

## Requirements
- Demonstrated experience leading software development teams.
- Demonstrated experience as a primary technical team leader / mentor.
- Demonstrated experience building web-client or server-side applications / services.
- Demonstrated experience with Agile development methodologies, including Test Driven Development and Continuous Integration / Deployment.
- Demonstrated experience with build and test pipeline automation, monitoring, and DevOps methodologies.
- Demonstrated experience with cloud computing platforms such as AWS or Microsoft Azure.

## Nice to Have
- Experience leading teams providing shared services for multiple teams.
- Experience as manager, including individual performance management, career development, and hiring.
- Experience with AI technologies and using them to solve business problems.
- Advanced Computer Science, Engineering, IT-related, or business-related degree.

## Evidence Rule
Do not soften the technical leadership requirements. This is a software engineering management role with AI/automation context, not only an AI transformation or adoption role.
