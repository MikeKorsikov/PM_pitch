# Case Retrospective - Procter & Gamble Senior Technical Product Manager - Pampers Club App

## Outcome
Rejected after application submission and logic-focused tests.

## What Worked
- Fast-track workflow was used appropriately for a live role.
- Job description was captured and role fit assessment was completed before materials.
- Role fit scoring identified the case as good_fit / proceed_with_caution.
- Application materials were tailored around technical product management, platform simplification, vendor challenge, and delivery governance.
- User validation improved the wording around vendor challenge and architecture simplification.

## What Did Not Work
- Application did not progress beyond assessment stage.
- Direct mobile app, loyalty platform, and customer-facing digital product evidence remained limited.
- The case relied on transferable technical product and enterprise delivery experience rather than direct consumer app ownership.
- Logic-focused assessment outcome may indicate a separate preparation need unrelated to the written application materials.

## Reusable Assets
- Senior Technical Product Manager role-fit logic.
- Platform simplification and right-sized architecture positioning.
- Vendor governance / commercial challenge language.
- CV bullets around delivery governance, cost/value trade-offs, and stakeholder alignment.
- Application narrative fragments for technical product, product governance, and pragmatic delivery roles.

## Weak Assumptions
- P&G may have weighted direct consumer mobile app or loyalty platform experience more heavily than the job description implied.
- Logic-focused tests may have carried significant screening weight.
- The application may have been acceptable, but not differentiated enough against candidates with direct mobile product experience.

## Repository Updates Needed
- Add assessment preparation tracking for roles requiring logic, reasoning, or aptitude tests.
- Add a field for assessment_required / assessment_completed / assessment_result in case config.
- Strengthen reusable positioning for technical product roles where the candidate lacks direct domain experience.
- Keep a reusable vendor challenge wording pattern, but avoid adversarial phrasing in employer-facing materials.

## Final Status
Rejected. Case closed for this opportunity and retained for reuse.
