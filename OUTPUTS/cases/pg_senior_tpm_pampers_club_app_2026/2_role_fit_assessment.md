## Role Summary
The role is a strong technical product management and platform simplification role. It focuses on rebuilding Pampers Club into a modular, globally reusable, scalable, maintainable, and cost-efficient platform. The emphasis is on business-technology translation, vendor challenge, architecture validation, delivery governance, standardization, and pragmatic decision-making rather than pure feature ownership or hands-on engineering.

## Key Requirements Extracted
- Lead rebuild of a customer-facing application into a modular global platform.
- Translate business needs into lean, standardized, scalable solutions.
- Challenge requests that add cost or complexity without clear value.
- Act as technical counterpart to a development agency.
- Validate architecture, estimates, and implementation choices.
- Manage planning, prioritization, timelines, and delivery governance.
- Explain trade-offs between value, speed, cost, scalability, and maintainability.
- Lead targeted PoCs before scaling.
- Understand APIs, architectures, integrations, and cloud environments.

## Seniority Fit
Aligned to stretch. The role expects senior TPM judgment, delivery ownership, vendor challenge, and technical trade-off management. The candidate has strong senior product/governance/transformation experience, but the consumer mobile app and direct app architecture depth are the main stretch areas.

## Underqualification Signals
- No direct consumer mobile application, customer-facing digital product, or loyalty platform ownership confirmed by user.
- Hands-on API, cloud, or mobile architecture implementation ownership should not be overstated.
- P&G / Pampers Club domain context is not evidenced beyond the job description.

## Overqualification Signals
- Candidate brings broader finance systems product governance, transformation, and enterprise operating model experience than a narrow app delivery role may require.
- Application should avoid sounding too enterprise-architecture-heavy; the role needs practical product delivery and cost simplification.

## Acceptable Gaps
- Lack of direct Pampers Club or P&G internal context.
- Consumer app domain gap, if positioned around transferable product governance, platform simplification, vendor governance, and delivery leadership.
- Deep technical implementation gap, if the role is TPM / technical counterpart rather than hands-on engineering lead.

## Red Flag Gaps
- If P&G requires direct prior ownership of a high-scale consumer mobile app rebuild.
- If P&G expects hands-on cloud/mobile architecture decision ownership beyond TPM-level technical challenge.

## Positioning Opportunities
- Position as a senior technical product and transformation lead who simplifies complex platforms and delivery models.
- Emphasize ability to challenge complexity, cost, and over-engineering.
- Use OnePlan / Board SaaS implementation as evidence of large-scale product ownership, vendor coordination, governance, and cost/value accountability.
- Use the AI-built CRM prototype carefully as practical evidence of user-centered product iteration and real business workflow translation.
- Frame architecture contribution as pragmatic architecture validation and trade-off leadership, not deep hands-on engineering.
- Use user-confirmed improvement themes carefully: fewer clicks to reach the desired state, simplified product/process structure, and more agile delivery with fewer approvers.
- Use vendor challenge as a strong theme: commercially aware scrutiny of vendor proposals, estimates, scope, complexity, and value for money.
- Avoid saying "monolith" as the headline in application material because the JD explicitly asks for a modular global platform. Use "right-sized architecture" and "avoid unnecessary microservice fragmentation" instead.
