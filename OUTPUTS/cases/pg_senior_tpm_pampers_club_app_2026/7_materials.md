## Vendor Challenge Clarification
For this application, "vendor challenge" means evidence that you did not simply accept supplier or agency proposals at face value. Useful examples include:
- asking a vendor to justify an estimate
- challenging why a proposed architecture or implementation was too complex
- pushing for a simpler or cheaper delivery option
- rejecting low-value scope
- comparing options based on value, speed, cost, risk, and maintainability
- escalating delivery, cost, or supportability concerns

Employer-safe wording:

```text
I bring a commercially aware approach to vendor governance. I do not accept supplier proposals at face value; I challenge estimates, scope, complexity, and implementation choices to make sure delivery decisions are justified by value, maintainability, and long-term cost.
```
