# Capability Impact Map - Volvo Group Digital Product Owner - HyperAutomation

## Purpose
Map selected pain point hypotheses to business capabilities, technology capabilities, and transformation opportunities.

## Pain Point to Capability Map
| Pain Point | Affected Business Capabilities | Affected Technology Capabilities | Product Owner Relevance |
|---|---|---|---|
| low_automation | HR operations, employee experience, workflow management, service management | workflow automation, rules engine, task routing, integration, identity/access | Prioritize automation backlog and maximize value. |
| inefficient_workflow | process management, continuous improvement, employee services | process modelling, workflow orchestration, SLA monitoring, document lifecycle tools | Discover bottlenecks and simplify employee journeys. |
| poor_e2e_visibility | service management, governance, operational reporting | dashboards, process monitoring, audit logs, workflow analytics | Improve transparency of requests, approvals, and outcomes. |
| fragmented_governance | product governance, compliance management, vendor governance | backlog tooling, approval workflows, documentation repositories | Clarify ownership, decision rights, and delivery controls. |

## Architecture Implications
- Hyperautomation products need clear process ownership, access controls, integration boundaries, auditability, and support model clarity.
- Power Platform and document/workflow tools should be governed as part of a reusable product landscape, not isolated local automations.
- Automation should simplify real work before scaling, rather than digitizing unclear processes.

## Transformation Opportunities
- Standardize recurring People & Culture workflows.
- Improve employee self-service and request handling.
- Reduce manual approval and document handoff effort.
- Use product discovery to prioritize the highest-value automation opportunities.
- Create reusable automation patterns with clear governance and adoption support.
