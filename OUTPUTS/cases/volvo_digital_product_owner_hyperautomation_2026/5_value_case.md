## Purpose
Connect HyperAutomation product ownership to business value using relevant value models.

## Selected Value Models

- operational_efficiency

## Business Outcomes
- Reduced manual effort in People & Culture workflows.
- Faster employee-facing processes.
- Better request, approval, and document lifecycle visibility.
- Improved product backlog prioritization based on business value and user impact.
- Better governance of automation demand, suppliers, changes, and reusable digital patterns.

- manual touch reduction
- request / approval turnaround time
- backlog throughput
- adoption rate
- user satisfaction
- SLA compliance
- exception rate
- support volume
