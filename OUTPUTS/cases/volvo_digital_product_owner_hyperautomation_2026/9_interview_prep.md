# Interview Preparation - Volvo Group Digital Product Owner - HyperAutomation

## Purpose
Prepare interview talking points if Volvo responds after application.

## Role-Fit Story
This role fits because it combines product ownership, backlog prioritization, process improvement, automation, stakeholder alignment, and employee experience. My relevant experience is strongest in owning enterprise platforms, improving ways of working, managing vendors and technical teams, and translating business needs into governed delivery.

## Key Talking Points
- Product ownership: roadmap, backlog, priorities, governance, stakeholders.
- Hyperautomation: start with process clarity and value, not tool-first automation.
- Employee experience: reduce friction, handoffs, approvals, and unclear ownership.
- Vendor/supplier coordination: challenge scope, estimates, complexity, and supportability.
- Agile delivery: keep priorities clear, manage backlog, improve flow, and use evidence for decisions.
- Governance: ensure access, auditability, ownership, controls, and support model are considered.

## Likely Questions
| Question | Suggested Answer Direction |
|---|---|
| Why this role? | It combines digital product ownership, workflow automation, process simplification, and employee experience. |
| What does hyperautomation mean to you? | Practical automation of repetitive, rules-based, document-heavy, or approval-heavy work, with governance and adoption built in. |
| How would you prioritize the backlog? | By value, user impact, risk, feasibility, reuse potential, and alignment with People & Culture strategy. |
| How do you avoid automating bad processes? | Map the current workflow, identify bottlenecks and ownership gaps, simplify first, automate second. |
| What is your Power Platform experience? | Answer honestly after user validation. |
| How do you work with suppliers? | Clear requirements, challenge estimates and complexity, track delivery, escalate early, and keep value for money visible. |
| What is your employee-experience experience? | Use Workday, people-management workflows, access/request/documentation/support process examples. |
