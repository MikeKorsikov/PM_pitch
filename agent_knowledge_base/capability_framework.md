# Enterprise Capability Framework

This framework represents the consolidated competency profile for Transformation Manager, Enterprise Business Architect, Process Architect, Technical Product Manager, Enterprise Architect, and Transformation Lead roles. It is optimized for downstream consumption by AI agents, capability mapping, role matching, transformation planning, and skill graphs.

## 1. Strategy & Transformation
* **Transformation Strategy & Roadmap Formulation**
  * Define enterprise transformation strategy and target operating models (TOM) aligned with business objectives.
  * Develop multi-year transformation roadmaps, sequencing work streams, and establishing key performance indicators (KPIs) and objectives and key results (OKRs).
* **Enterprise Integration & Impact Analysis**
  * Analyze cross-functional dependencies ("connecting the dots") to align business and technology domains.
  * Conduct business impact assessments to identify risks and operational friction points across business units.
* **Financial & Portfolio Management**
  * Execute project budgeting, capital expenditure (CAPEX), and operational expenditure (OPEX) forecasting.
  * Perform cost-benefit analyses (CBA) and total cost of ownership (TCO) calculations for enterprise transformation initiatives.
* **Continuous Improvement & Operational Excellence**
  * Design, implement, and optimize agile and lean operating models to improve organizational ways of working.
  * Lead change management and process maturity assessments to drive continuous improvement.

## 2. Business Architecture
* **Business Capability Modeling**
  * Design and maintain enterprise business capability maps to define what the business does.
  * Map business capabilities to value streams, application landscapes (using SAP LeanIX), and strategic objectives.
* **Requirement Elicitation & Elaboration**
  * Facilitate structured business workshops to elicit, clarify, and document complex business requirements.
  * Translate high-level strategic objectives into structured epics, features, and detailed requirements.
* **Business Architecture Repository Management**
  * Catalog and govern business architecture metadata, assets, and diagrams within repository tools (e.g., SAP LeanIX).

## 3. Process Excellence & Automation
* **Process Modeling & Mapping**
  * Design end-to-end business process flow diagrams adhering to Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN 2.0) standards (using Camunda Modeler, Bizagi Modeler, Lucidchart, MS Visio).
  * Define hierarchical process architectures (levels 1 through 5) spanning core, management, and support processes.
* **Process Discovery & Reverse Engineering**
  * Reverse engineer undocumented, legacy, and "as-is" operational processes using observational and data-driven methods (e.g., process mining).
  * Identify process bottlenecks, redundancies, and automation opportunities.
* **Process Standardization & Design**
  * Author and implement standard operating procedures (SOPs) and global templates to eliminate local process variance.
  * Design optimized "to-be" process flows incorporating automated decision logic and workflows.

## 4. Product & Delivery Management
* **Product Lifecycle & Strategy Management**
  * Define product vision, strategy, and roadmap priorities based on user needs and business value (using Aha!).
  * Manage product release plans and product-market fit metrics.
* **Agile Product Delivery Execution**
  * Manage backlog refinement, user story definition, sprint planning, and team delivery velocity (using Jira).
  * Coordinate code reviews, version control branching, and source code management (using Git, GitHub, GitLab).
* **Requirement Lifecycle Management**
  * Trace requirement implementation status from initial elicitation to testing, deployment, and operational verification.

## 5. Enterprise Architecture & Technology
* **Enterprise Application & Integration Architecture**
  * Design, map, and govern enterprise application portfolios, integration patterns, and data flows (focused on SAP ECC, SAP Fiori, Azure).
  * Align application portfolios with business capability models using enterprise architecture tools (e.g., SAP LeanIX).
* **Legacy System Archaeology & Reverse Engineering**
  * Analyze legacy codebases, relational schemas, and system integrations to reverse engineer system architecture and business logic.
  * Define migration, decommissioning, or modernizing paths for legacy IT assets.
* **Cloud Infrastructure Architecture**
  * Design and configure cloud compute, storage, and architectural services using Microsoft Azure.
* **Data Engineering & Query Optimization**
  * Query and analyze relational databases using SQL to perform data profiling, migrations, and schema design.
* **Task Automation & Scripting**
  * Automate administrative, data extraction, and integration tasks using Python and PowerShell.

## 6. Stakeholder & Governance Management
* **Stakeholder Engagement & Communication Planning**
  * Formulate communication plans and manage stakeholder expectations across diverse business units and executive leadership.
  * Prepare steering committee reports, business cases, and strategic status briefs.
* **Vendor & Partner Governance**
  * Run Request for Proposal (RFP) processes, evaluate vendor bids, and monitor Service Level Agreement (SLA) compliance.
  * Coordinate external contingent workforce and vendor deliverables (using SAP Fieldglass).
* **Enterprise IT Service & Change Governance**
  * Enforce enterprise change control, release management, and IT service request workflows (using SAP ChaRM, ServiceNow, SAP Solution Manager).
* **Risk, Compliance & Identity Governance**
  * Enforce privileged access management (PAM), identity governance, and enterprise risk compliance controls (using CyberArk, SailPoint, Archer).
* **Resource Capacity Management**
  * Forecast, model, and allocate project staffing resources (using Workday).
* **Controlled Document & Repository Governance**
  * Govern the creation, verification, audit trails, and archival of controlled documents and knowledge bases (using Veeva Vault, SAP Solution Manager, Confluence, SharePoint).

## 7. Data, AI & Digital Enablement
* **AI-Assisted Development & Prompt Engineering**
  * Maximize development efficiency by executing prompt design, code generation, and workspace analysis using developer-oriented AI agents (Claude Code, Antigravity, GitHub Copilot, Microsoft Copilot, OpenAI Codex) and large language models (GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek).
* **Data Analytics & Business Intelligence**
  * Design, build, and publish analytical dashboards and reports for business performance tracking (using SAP Analytics Cloud [SAC], PowerBI, Microsoft Excel).
